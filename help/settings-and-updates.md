# Settings and updates

[Help contents](README.md)

## Updates

Vedra Opus never looks for updates unless you ask it to.

In the menu (☰), under **Help**:

- **Check for Updates…** looks once, now.
- **Check for Updates Automatically:** tick it, and Vedra Opus looks when it starts and once a day.
- **Update Channel:** **Stable** for finished releases, or **Beta** for test releases such as alphas and betas.

**When an update is found,** you are told, and choose whether to download it. Once downloaded, it installs when you close the app, or straight away if you choose.

- **Windows** and the **Linux AppImage** update in place.
- **macOS** and the **Linux .deb:** download the new version from the [downloads page](https://github.com/lpgeach/vedra-opus-releases/releases) and install it over the old one.

Your projects and settings are kept when you update.

## Remembered settings

Vedra Opus remembers, for next time:

- the size and place of the window;
- the panels' sizes, which are shown, and the ribbon collapsed or not;
- the theme (**View → System**, **Light** or **Dark**);
- your recent projects, player presets and templates.

**View → Reset Layout** puts the panels back as they were at first.

## Third-party notices

**Help → Third-Party Notices…** lists the open-source software and media Vedra Opus includes, with their licences: the libraries it is built with, ffmpeg and LibreOffice (with where their source code is), the icon and illustration libraries, and the demo course's music and pictures.

Vedra Opus itself is free software, under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later; its source code is at [lpgeach/vedra-opus-source](https://github.com/lpgeach/vedra-opus-source). **Help → Project Repository** opens the downloads page.

## Crash reports

If Vedra Opus crashes, it can keep a report on your computer, to help find the cause.

- **Help → Keep Crash Reports:** tick it to keep them. It is off until you tick it, and takes effect from the next start.
- **Help → Show Crash Reports** opens the folder they are kept in.

Reports stay on your computer: Vedra Opus never sends them anywhere. To share one, attach it to your feedback.

## Sending feedback

**Help → Send Feedback…** opens the feedback page in your browser, with the app's version and your computer's system filled in. Report a problem, suggest something, or ask a question, with screenshots if they help.

## Privacy

Vedra Opus does not collect anything about you or your work: no tracking, no usage data. It reaches the internet only when you ask it to: checking for updates, opening a link, sending feedback in your browser. Your courses, once published, talk only to the LMS or LRS they are launched from.

## Supporting Vedra Opus

**Donate**, at the top right of the window (or **Help → Donate…**), opens the page to support its development.
