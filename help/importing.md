# Importing

[Help contents](README.md)

You can start a course from a PowerPoint deck or an Articulate Storyline course, copy slides from another Vedra Opus project, or start from a template.

## PowerPoint

**Import PowerPoint…** (on the Welcome screen, or **File → Import PowerPoint…**) makes a new course from a `.pptx` file.

**What comes across:**

- every slide in order, with its title and its notes;
- the deck's masters, layouts and backgrounds, as Vedra Opus masters and layouts;
- text, with its formatting;
- shapes, where Vedra Opus has the same shape, and pictures, with their crop and their alt text;
- tables;
- transitions and animations, where they have a match;
- sound and video.

**Charts, SmartArt and anything else Vedra Opus cannot rebuild** are drawn as pictures, so the slide still looks right. Vedra Opus includes a cut-down copy of LibreOffice to draw them.

**The import report:** when the import finishes, a report lists each slide's items: what was imported, what was approximated, and what was drawn as a picture. **Copy report** copies it, to keep or send.

The new course opens unsaved: save it to keep it.

## Articulate Storyline

**Import Storyline Course…** (on the Welcome screen, or **File → Import Articulate Storyline Course…**) makes a new course from a Storyline `.story` file, so you can move courses you made there.

**What comes across:** scenes and slides, with their timing; text, shapes, pictures and callouts; layers; states; triggers and variables; sound, video and captions; animations and motion paths; buttons, sliders, dials and hotspots; tables; quiz questions, feedback layers and result slides; drag-and-drop questions; question banks.

**While it imports,** a cover over the window shows how far it has got: the stage and the file being converted. Large courses with a lot of media can take a few minutes.

**The import report** lists anything brought across differently, slide by slide. Check an imported course before you rely on it: preview it, and go through the report.

The new course opens unsaved: save it to keep it.

## Slides from another project

**Slides → Import Slides…** (or **File → Import Slides…**) copies slides from another Vedra Opus project into this one, with everything on them: their layers, triggers, media and the variables they use.

## Templates

A **template** is a project to start new courses from.

- **New from Template…** (on the Welcome screen, or **File → New from Template…**) starts a new course from a template:
  - the **starter templates** that come with Vedra Opus;
  - the demo course;
  - your own templates.
- **File → Save as Template…** keeps the open project as one of your templates. Your templates are kept on this computer.
- **Slides → From Template…** adds slides from a template to the course you are working on.

A course started from a template is a new, unsaved copy: the template itself never changes.
