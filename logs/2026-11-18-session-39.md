## Done

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P6.4-captions.md`, written before the code and updated at the end. Import replaces without asking (as one undo step, with a notice), and the time fields have editing rules.
- **Saved format 8 (model):**
  - `props.captions` on audio and video: `{ id, start, end, text }` in the media file's own seconds.
  - Schema: `start < end`. Validation: cue ids unique project-wide, and no cue ending after the media.
  - Migration 7 → 8 adds an empty list to every audio and video object, at any depth, on slides, layers, masters and layouts. Legacy fixture `fixtures/legacy/v7-two-slides`; the project fixtures were bumped to 8.
- **Captions text:** `packages/model/src/captions.ts` handles WebVTT and SubRip.
  - **`parseCaptions` accepts:** a BOM, CRLF, cue identifiers, `MM:SS.mmm`, and SRT's comma.
  - **It ignores:** cue settings and NOTE/STYLE/REGION blocks.
  - **Markup and entities:** markup is removed and entities are decoded.
  - **Bad input:** unreadable cues are skipped and reported with their line numbers. A file with none is an error.
  - **Writing:** `formatVtt` and `formatSrt`.
- **Engine:** `MediaView.caption` is the text of the cues active at the media's file time. Overlapping cues are joined with line breaks, and a video's time is offset by its trim start. It is null when the media is stopped, completed or still waiting for its place, and it keeps showing while the media is paused.
- **Player:**
  - a **CC** button in the footer, only when the course has captions;
  - off by default, and `aria-pressed`;
  - the choice lasts across slides;
  - a caption band at the bottom of the stage, `aria-hidden`.
- **Desktop:**
  - IPC `project:openCaptions` (an open dialog, VTT/SRT, 2 MB at most) and `project:saveCaptions` (SRT for a `.srt` name, otherwise VTT).
  - A **Captions** tab in the bottom panel for the selected sound or video:
    - the waveform, with cue blocks and a cursor (clicking moves the cursor and chooses the cue under it);
    - Play and Pause, with the cursor following playback;
    - rows for start, end and text, with Delete;
    - Add caption at the cursor;
    - Import and Export.
  - Every edit is an `object.update`, so undo and redo work.
- **Found in review, before the e2e test:** the time fields apply as they are typed, and the list re-sorted on every keystroke. The row being typed in moved, and its field lost focus. Typing a start digit by digit also pushed the end out and lost it. Now:
  - each field clamps only its own end;
  - the rows hold their places while a time field has focus (`held`), and the saved list stays sorted.
  - I checked that the new e2e assertion fails without the hold: "toBeFocused … Received: inactive".

## Mistakes this session, and what changed

- **Literal BOM and no-break space in source.** The parser was written through a heredoc with real U+FEFF and U+00A0 characters in it. Lint (`no-irregular-whitespace`) caught them; they are now `﻿` and ` `. Recorded in pitfall #90.
- **Expected values worked out in my head (rule 12 again).** Two first drafts were wrong: an SRT block's line number and the caption expected at 2.6 s in an engine test. I recomputed both by hand, and the code was right each time.
- **The brief said "ask before replacing" while the code did not.** The code (one undo step plus a notice) is the better behaviour, so the brief was corrected at the end. Next time, change the brief when the design changes, not afterwards.
- **A list re-sorting under a focused field** (above): found by reading the code before writing the e2e test, not by a test. Pitfall #90 now warns about any sorted list a `NumericInput` edits.
- **An ambiguous `getByRole('status')`:** the conversion progress is also a status region. The notice now has its own test id.
