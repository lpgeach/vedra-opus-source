## What was done

- **Model, format 28:**
  - `slider` and `dial` objects: range, step, initial value, a number variable, a colour and a track colour, and a slider's orientation;
  - the event `controlMoves`;
  - validation: least below greatest, the step within the range, the start inside it, a number variable, and the event's object a slider or dial;
  - removing a variable or changing its type unbinds them (P9.2's `unbindFields`, widened);
  - `createSliderObject`, `createDialObject` and `snapToStep`;
  - migration 27 → 28 changes nothing; legacy fixture `v27-two-slides`.
- **Engine:**
  - `moveControl(id, value)` snaps and clamps; a new value writes a bound variable, then raises `controlMoves`;
  - the view's `controlValue` follows a bound variable, so triggers move the control;
  - unbound controls keep their own value.
- **Render:**
  - pictures of both at their initial value, in the editor and thumbnails;
  - in a course, a slider is a native range input, and a dial is SVG with `role="slider"` and its ARIA values.
- **Player (`packages/player/src/controls.ts`):** keeps both at the engine's value. Sliders move by the browser's own input; dials by drag (`dialValue`) and by the arrow, page, Home and End keys (`keyedValue`). None of these keys changes slides.
- **Editor:**
  - Insert → Data Entry → Slider and Dial;
  - the Format panel's control section: variable, least, greatest, step, starts at, direction, and the two colours. Changes that would make an invalid control are not applied.
  - The trigger wizard's "a slider or dial moves".

## Found and fixed

- **`snapToStep` rounded a value exactly halfway the wrong way:** 0.35 with steps of 0.1 gave 0.3, because 0.35 / 0.1 is 3.4999999999999996. The step count is now cleared to nine decimal places before rounding. The test found it.
