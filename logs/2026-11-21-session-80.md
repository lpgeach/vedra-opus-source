This is the last part of P10.1 (`docs/briefs/P10.1-accessibility.md`). The brief's "Part C as designed" section, written first, settles what the plan left open:

- what the text view holds;
- where the learner's settings are kept;
- how a faint accent is handled.

## Found before changing anything

- **The player's control borders fell short of 3 : 1:**
  - dark: 2.80 against the background, 2.41 on the menu and panels;
  - light: 2.72 against the background.

  Every text colour already passed (11.5 to 16.4 : 1).
- **A custom accent was used as it was,** however faint. On the light scheme, the yellow `#ffd23f` gave focus rings and the current menu entry 1.44 : 1 against the chrome, and 1.27 : 1 against the background.
- **The player remembered nothing for the learner:** the caption size went back to Normal on every visit.

## What was done

- **The player's colours, in the model as well:**
  - `PLAYER_SCHEMES`, `contrastRatio`, `accentFor` and `UI_CONTRAST` in `packages/model/src/player-look.ts`;
  - the borders darkened (light, `#808a93`) or lightened (dark, `#6e7780`) just enough to reach 3 : 1.
- **The accent as a course shows it:**
  - kept when it reaches 3 : 1 against the scheme's background, chrome and sheets;
  - otherwise mixed toward the scheme's text colour until it does;
  - one shade for each scheme, so `auto` follows the learner's system in CSS.

  The Player dialog says when a course will show a stronger shade, and names it.
- **The text view** (`packages/player/src/text-view.ts`): "Text view" and "Text size" (100 to 200 %) in the accessibility panel. It is an `aside` named "Slide text", beside the slide, or below it when the player is narrow. It holds:
  - the title;
  - each shown layer's objects in reading order (the author's Tab order first, then by layout);
  - paragraphs and lists;
  - pictures by their alt text;
  - tables with headers;
  - fields with what is typed in them;
  - choices, the chosen one marked.

  It follows states, layers, variables and feedback messages. Decoration and hidden objects give nothing, as for a screen reader; a question's answers and drag tiles are always read.
- **Remembered settings** (`packages/player/src/settings.ts`): caption size, text view and text size, kept in the browser's `localStorage` under the course's id. They never leave the machine. Where storage is refused, the settings last for the visit.
- Seven new label keys: `textView`, `textSize`, `slideText`, `textPicture`, `textField`, `textEmpty`, `textChosen`.

## Tests

- **Model** (`contrast.test.ts`):
  - every text pair of both schemes at 4.5 : 1;
  - borders and focus rings at 3 : 1;
  - `accentFor` for 216 accents in both schemes, with 4.5 : 1 text on each;
  - the default accent kept as it is.
- **Player unit:**
  - `text-view.test.ts`: rich text, reading order, the Tab order, groups, states, hidden objects, layers, typed text, variables, tables, choices, and the drawn elements;
  - `settings.test.ts`: saved and loaded per course, junk ignored, refused storage;
  - `look.test.ts`: the per-scheme accents.
- **Player browser:**
  - `a11y.spec.ts`: the drawn colours of each scheme (and `auto` on light and dark systems) are the model's, and a faint accent's focus ring is the strengthened shade;
  - `text-view.spec.ts`: the content and its order, a layer shown, the next slide, 200 % text, remembered across a reload, below the slide and wrapping at 320 pixels wide with the WCAG 1.4.12 spacing, and axe-core finding nothing with the view on.
- **e2e:** `player-look.spec.ts` checks the dialog's note.

**One existing expectation changed:** `player-look.spec.ts` expected the published course's current menu entry to be exactly `#ffd23f` on the light scheme. That yellow is too faint there, so the course now shows `accentFor('#ffd23f', 'light')`, and the test expects that shade.
