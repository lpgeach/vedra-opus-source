## Done

- **Grow box to fit** applies to any change to a box's text, padding, autofit or width, not only on-stage edits, and the box shrinks as well as grows (pitfall #60).
- **Theme audit** (Luke: dialogs such as the theme colours and fonts did not follow dark mode). Cause: undefined CSS variables with white fallbacks, and unstyled inputs and dropdowns. Every control now takes its colours from the theme tokens (pitfall #61). Reviewed screenshots of 14 screens in both themes.
- **Check of everything so far**, fixing what turned up:
  - Format panel and Timeline number fields ignored typing while a text box was being edited; all number fields are now one component and each entry is one undo step (pitfall #62).
  - Stage Size: clearing a field put "1" back (so 640 became 1640); blank or zero sizes could be applied.
  - Menu bar Insert items (Text Box, Rectangle, Picture, Button) were dead placeholders; they work.
  - A shape with no text had no Text Format tab; it has, and formatting an empty box starts typing in it with that format (pitfall #63).
  - Nested undo groups threw; they now join.
- `e2e/audit.spec.ts` (6 tests) covers all of the above.
