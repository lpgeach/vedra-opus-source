# Session 54 — 2026-11-19

## What this session was for

P7.6, question banks and draws: banks; draw N questions at random or in order; lock specific questions into a draw.

Worked on the Windows machine straight after session 53 (P7.5, merged in PR #58), with the Linux checks in WSL.

Luke said to continue without being asked anything, so the design choices are mine. They are listed at the top of `docs/briefs/P7.6-banks-and-draws.md`:

- banks are shaped like scenes and hold question slides only;
- the draw is made once per session and saved;
- a bank slide is drawn at most once per session;
- locked questions are always drawn;
- "random" shuffles the whole draw;
- a jump to a draw slide goes to its first question.

There is no AI attribution in any commit or in the PR.

## Done

On `wp/p7.6-banks`: the brief, then steps A, B and C, and one fix commit.

### Step A: the model (format 18)

- **The format:** `project.banks` and `slide.draw` (bank, count, order, locked).
- **Traversal:**
  - `allSlides` stays the course's scene slides;
  - `everySlide` adds the banks' slides, for validation, ids, layers and finding;
  - `findScene`, `findSlide`, `allLayers` and `idsInProject` look in banks too.
- **Validation:**
  - bank slides must be questions;
  - draw slides must be in scenes and not questions;
  - a draw's bank and locked slides must exist;
  - jumps can name only scene slides.
- **Commands:**
  - `bank.add`, `bank.remove` and `bank.rename`, and `slide.setDraw`;
  - `slide.add` and `slide.move` into a bank take question slides only;
  - `slide.setQuestion` cannot take the question off a bank slide, or give one to a draw slide;
  - removing a bank leaves its draws with `bank: null`;
  - removing a bank slide, or moving it out of its bank, unlocks it.
- **Migration 17 → 18,** and the legacy fixture `v17-two-slides`.

### Step B: the engine and the player

- **`drawQuestions` and `drawAll`** (in `packages/engine/src/draw.ts`, pure).
- **The course order:** the engine plays each draw slide's questions in its place. A draw slide's id maps to where its questions start.
- **Session state:** `SessionState.d` holds the draws.
- **The suspend codec:**
  - it now indexes every slide, the banks' too (unchanged for a project with no banks);
  - it carries the draws.
- **The player and publishing:**
  - the player finds bank slides;
  - a bank slide previews on its own;
  - the SCORM 1.2 suspend estimate counts draws.

### Step C: the editor

- **The slide list's Question Banks section:** New Question Bank, a name field, Delete, and the banks' slides.
- **Insert → Question:** with a bank slide current, it adds to that bank. Its new item, "Draw from Question Bank", adds a draw slide, and a bank if there is none.
- **The Draw form:** bank, count, order and locked questions. Slide View says what a draw slide stands for.

### Changed from the brief

Banks are managed in the slide list, not in Overview. The brief now says so.
