**Date**: 2026-11-18
**Participant**: Luke + AI Assistant

Luke, on the question the Phase 6 review raised: "implement the reduce motion settings before we continue".

## Done

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/reduced-motion.md`, written first.
- **When it applies:** if the learner's system asks for less motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), read once as the course starts, or if `PlayerOptions.reducedMotion` forces it. This covers preview and published courses. The editor's stage is not affected.
- **What changes:**
  - **entrance and exit effects** (all twelve): a fade of the same length;
  - **motion paths:** the object is shown at the path's end as the run starts;
  - **slide and layer transitions** (all eight): the new one fades in over the old;
  - **zoom regions:** a cut, with no travel.
  - Timing, triggers (`animation completes` on time) and plan §5 are unchanged.
- **How:**
  - **Engine:** `EngineOptions.reducedMotion` changes only the view. `shownOffsetOf` gives the drawn position, while runs, relative starts and the saved `m` keep the real position, and the camera speed is 0.
  - **Render:** `{ reduced }` on `effectKeyframes`, `createAnimator`, `transitionKeyframes` and `layerTransitionKeyframes`.
  - **Player:** reads the setting, passes it to all of these, and marks its root `data-reduced-motion="true"`.

## Tests

- **Engine:** `reduced-motion.test.ts` (3 tests):
  - the path's object is shown at its end at once, while the saved position mid-run is the real one (halfway), and the run finishes on time;
  - the camera cuts in at the region's start and out at its end;
  - nothing changes without the option.
- **Render:** every effect kind is a fade to the object's own opacity, and every transition kind is a fade with nothing outgoing.
- **Player browser:** `reduced-motion.spec.ts` (2 tests, Chromium and Firefox), with `emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })`:
  - a fly-in stays in place and fades;
  - the path's object is at its end at tick 0;
  - the zoom is exactly ×2 just after the region starts;
  - the push to slide 2 leaves both slides in place, with the new one fading and nothing animating the old;
  - with `no-preference`, the same course moves.
