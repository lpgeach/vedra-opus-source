## Found and fixed

- **Previewing one slide could make an invalid project.** `scopeProject` (Preview → This Slide) kept three things naming slides it had left out:
  - tracking by a result;
  - a result's chosen slides;
  - a question's branches.

  It promised "a valid project on its own"; previewing a question in a result-tracked course broke that. It now drops those references, for bank slides too. The test fails without the fix.
- **Question branches could name any slide, and kept naming deleted ones.** This gap had been open since P7.1.
  - Validation now requires a branch to go to a slide of the course, not a bank slide.
  - Removing a slide, a scene or a bank sends branches to it on to the next slide.
- **Question choices were not accessible.** In a course, a choice had no role, no name and no checked state, and could not be answered from the keyboard; Likert points had no words at all. The player now makes the marks a question is answered with:
  - radio buttons (in a set) or checkboxes, with `aria-checked`;
  - named by their words, a Likert point by its statement and scale point ("First statement: Agree");
  - in the tab order, chosen with Space or Enter.

  There is a new browser test (Chromium and Firefox), and the Phase 7 e2e answers by these names.
- **The player's bar ran its labels together:** "Untitled ProjectTime left 10:00", and the review banner sat against the title. The bar is now a row with gaps.
- **Feedback colours were back to front for the default theme:** Correct was outlined amber and Incorrect teal. New feedback masters now use accent2 (green-blue) for Correct, accent4 (red) for Incorrect and accent3 (amber) for Try Again. Projects migrated from format 16 keep the frozen v17 design, as migrations must not change.
- **The feedback master's message was empty, so an author could not see it in Master View.** It now says "The feedback words go here."; each question's layer replaces the words.
- **The Draw form allowed a draw bigger than its bank without saying so.** It now says how many can be drawn.
