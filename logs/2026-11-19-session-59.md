## What was done

- **Model, format 22:**
  - `project.player.look`: scheme (dark, light, auto), accent, font (system, or the theme's body or heading), controls under or above, menu left or right;
  - `project.player.labels`: the course language and, per language, only the words the author changed;
  - `packages/model/src/labels.ts`: `DEFAULT_LABELS` (57 words, the English), `LABEL_KEYS`, `LANGUAGE_TAG` and `labelText`;
  - `packages/model/src/player-look.ts`: the defaults, `presetOf`, `withPreset` and `textOnAccent`;
  - `playerPresetSchema`; id prefix `preset`;
  - migration 21 → 22; legacy fixture `v21-two-slides`.
- **Player:**
  - `applyLook` on the root and on the resume prompt;
  - every colour in `player.css` is now a variable, with a dark set (as before) and a light set; `auto` follows `prefers-color-scheme`;
  - controls at the top, and the menu at the right (the tab panel then at the left);
  - every word the player shows comes from `labelText`, the results print page and the loading screen included. The English output is unchanged.
  - The buttons in the bar (Menu, the tabs) now have the frame's button style. Before, they had the browser's default.
  - Links in the tab panel have a colour for each scheme.
- **Editor:**
  - Player dialog → **Look:** scheme, accent, font, controls, menu side; presets (Apply, Delete, Save as preset).
  - Player dialog → **Words:** the course language, and every word with its English beside it.
  - Presets are kept in `settings.json` (`Settings.playerPresets`). A damaged preset is dropped on load, and the rest are kept.
