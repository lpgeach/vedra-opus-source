## What was done

- **Dragging by keyboard** (WCAG 2.1.1 and 2.5.7), in `packages/player/src/player.ts` (`dragKey`):
  - a draggable object, or a drag question's tile, is a Tab stop and a button, named and described by a hidden hint;
  - Space or Enter picks it up;
  - the arrow keys move it from target to target, each announced ("Over Fruit."). With no targets (a free object that no trigger names a target for), they move it 10 pixels, or 1 with Shift;
  - Space or Enter drops it, and Escape or Tab puts it back ("Cancelled.").
  - The drop is the engine's own `drop`, so triggers, states, snapping and marking are exactly the pointer's.
  - Six new label keys: `dragHint`, `dragPickedUp`, `dragOver`, `dragStart`, `dragDropped`, `dragCancelled`.
- **`engine.dropTargets(id)`**, the targets the keys go through:
  - a word bank's blank;
  - a matching question's targets;
  - a sequence's or ranking's other tiles;
  - a freeform drag question's targets;
  - for a free object, the shown targets its "dragged over" and "dropped on" triggers name, once each, in trigger order;
  - nothing once the question is finished.
- **A slide's Tab order**, format 35:
  - `slide.properties.focusOrder` holds object ids, or `null` for the layout's order;
  - a migration from 34 sets `null`, and there is a legacy fixture (`fixtures/legacy/v34-two-slides`);
  - validation: only the slide's own objects, each once;
  - removing an object, ungrouping, or removing a layer takes the objects out (`pruneFocusOrder`).
  - `tabbableObjects` and `tabOrder` in `packages/model/src/tab-order.ts`. The author's list comes first, then anything not in it, by layout (top to bottom in rows, then left to right), so a new button is never left out.
- **The Slide panel's "Tab order"** (`TabOrder.tsx`): "Follow the layout" or "My own order", and the list of objects with "Move … earlier/later" buttons. The brief allowed dragging or buttons; buttons work by keyboard and screen reader as they are.
- **In a course** the player moves focus itself on Tab and Shift+Tab within the slide (`tabStops`, `tabKey`), in that order:
  - after the last stop, Tab goes to the controls;
  - Shift+Tab from the controls lands on the last stop;
  - the skip link goes to the first stop.
  - The drawing order is unchanged.

## Found while testing, and fixed

- **The drag hint was a second `.vo-player-announcer`,** so the announcement tests found two. It has its own class (`.vo-player-sr-only`).
- **Tab past the last stop stayed in the slide**, because the browser's own order went back into the stage. The player now focuses the next focusable element after the stage, and when focus comes in from outside it goes to the first or last stop.

## Tests

- **Engine:** `dropTargets` for a free object (`drag.test.ts`), for the word bank, matching and sequence kinds and after finishing (`graded.test.ts`), and for freeform drag (`freeform.test.ts`).
- **Model:** `focus-order.test.ts` covers validation, the undo step, pruning on remove, ungroup and layer removal, the migration, and which objects are reached in what order. The undo property test has a `focusOrderSet` step.
- **Player browser:**
  - `drag.spec.ts`: keyboard dragging of a free object to a target and by steps, and Escape;
  - `graded.spec.ts`: a word bank, a matching and a sequence question answered by keyboard, each marked correct, as the pointer test's are;
  - `freeform.spec.ts`: freeform drag by keyboard, with Drag Over, the drop states, snapping, the announcements and a correct mark;
  - `a11y.spec.ts`: Tab follows the layout, then the author's order.
- **e2e:** `e2e/tab-order.spec.ts` sets the author's order in the Slide panel, saves, reopens it in a fresh app, publishes, and Tabs through the course in that order.
