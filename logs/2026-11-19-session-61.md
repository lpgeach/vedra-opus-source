## What was done

- **Model, format 24:** only the label words `more`, `rotateHint` and `dismiss`.
  - Migration 23 → 24 changes nothing.
  - Legacy fixture `v23-two-slides`.
- **Player (`packages/player/src/responsive.ts`):**
  - **Compact below 640 pixels** (`data-compact`):
    - the title shortens with an ellipsis, and the tab buttons scroll sideways;
    - the menu and the tab panels cover the course;
    - the seekbar, volume, speed, accessibility and full screen sit behind **More** (`aria-expanded`), on a row above Previous and Next.
  - **Touch targets** of at least 44 pixels on coarse pointers.
  - **Touch swipes** for Next and Previous:
    - by the arrow keys' rules (a hidden button does nothing; in a lightbox, only with its controls);
    - never from the mouse;
    - not when the swipe starts on a drag tile, a field or a frame panel.
  - **Orientation:**
    - `data-orientation`;
    - for a landscape course in a portrait compact player, a hint to turn the device, with Dismiss, which puts it away for the page.
  - The viewport has `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom`.
- **No editor changes:** P8.5 adds no settings.
