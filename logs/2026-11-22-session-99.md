## What was done

- **File → Export Video…:**
  - the dialog shows the run-through's length and size;
  - Export WebM… asks where to save, then makes the video with a progress bar and a Cancel button;
  - the file appears only when complete.
- **The run-through:**
  - each slide in course order (no lightbox-only slides or banks), for its timeline;
  - the real player draws it, so animations, transitions and motion paths play;
  - sounds that play with their timelines are mixed in at their places;
  - branches, questions and clicks are not taken.
- **How:**
  - the course is hosted as Preview hosts it (Previews can now serve more than one course);
  - it plays in a hidden, muted, offscreen window, on a clock main sets, ticked frame by frame and captured;
  - frames are piped to ffmpeg, with the sounds placed by `adelay` and mixed by `amix`, into WebM;
  - the page's media never play, so the engine follows the clock alone and nothing can be heard.
- **Checked by eye:**
  - frames at 1 s (the first slide's shape) and 2.8 s (the second slide);
  - the sound's level: silent on the first slide, the tone on the second.
  - The first try caught a one-pixel line of the player's frame at the top; the stage's box is now rounded inwards.

## Tests
