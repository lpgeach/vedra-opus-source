## Done

- **Model (format 7):**
  - `video` objects: asset, length, trim start and end, volume, start (with the timeline, when clicked, or by a trigger), controls, picture-in-picture (off by default), poster (an image, or the first frame).
  - Validation: the trim must lie within the video, the asset must be a video, the poster a picture.
  - Media actions and `mediaCompletes` take video too.
  - Migration 6 → 7 and legacy fixture `v6-two-slides`. The property test adds videos with a trigger.
- **Engine:**
  - Video is media with P6.1's states. The played length is the trimmed one.
  - Start-on-click plays and pauses on a click, except when the video shows its controls (below).
  - `mediaControl()` takes the learner's use of the controls. The time master can be audio or video.
  - **New, for audio too:** the stall limit `MAX_STALL` (2 s; below).
- **Render:** a video draws as `<video>` with its poster or the frame at its trim start. In a course it gets controls and picture-in-picture as set (`liveMedia`).
- **Player:**
  - The board drives the stage's video elements, mapping trim to element time.
  - It detects the learner's controls by state each frame.
  - Redraws keep a playing video's element.
- **Editor:**
  - Insert → Video… and Media panel → Insert. The video is measured by its metadata; a file with no duration is measured by seeking to its end. It is placed at its own size, fitted to the stage.
  - Format panel → Video: volume, starts, controls, picture-in-picture, trim, poster, length.
  - The trigger wizard lists video with the media.
  - **Fix for P6.1:** inserting media longer than the timeline now lengthens the timeline, in the same undo step. A long narration on a 5 s slide used to stop at 5 s.
- **Tests:**
  - Engine: `video.test.ts` (6) and the stall test in `media.test.ts`.
  - Model: validation and the property step.
  - Render: unit tests and a visual test (`video-poster.png`).
  - Player browser tests: `video.spec.ts`, 4 tests in Chromium and Firefox. They cover trim, click to play, controls (pause, play, seek), the timeline following the video, and a state change not restarting it.
  - e2e: `video.spec.ts`. It covers inserting the video (size, length), trim, start on click, controls, poster, narration lengthening the timeline (with undo and redo), save and reopen, and publish and play.
  - `fixtures/make-media.ts` makes the test WAV and WebM at run time.

## Found while building, and fixed

- **The course froze forever on media that cannot play on.** In Firefox, a WebM recorded by Chromium has no cues and sticks after a seek (`readyState` 1). Because media is the time master, the timeline waited with it. This applies to audio too, our own narration recordings included.
  - A master that reports without moving on now holds its timeline for at most 2 s; then the clock takes over until the media moves again.
  - Both briefs say so.
- **Learner presses on the controls were lost.** The board reacted to `play` and `pause` events, which arrive after its own next frame may already have undone the press. It now compares each video with what it last left it doing.
- **Clicking a video with controls played it and paused it at once.** The browser toggles it, and the engine's start-on-click toggled it too. The engine now leaves clicks on a video with controls to the browser.
- **A test timeout too tight for Firefox under load.** The trimmed-video test waited 8 s for completion that takes about 9.7 s in Firefox, through the stall limit. The wait is now 15 s; the assertions are unchanged.

## Not done or not verified

- **Real-world video files:** no mp4, and no WebM from a camera or encoder, was tried. Only the WebM recorded in Chromium was used. Playwright's Chromium cannot play H.264, so mp4 in tests waits for P6.3 or for real browsers.

## Mistakes this session, and what changed

- **A dangerous command.** To empty a scratch folder I ran `cd "$T" && rm -rf *`. If the `cd` had failed, it would have deleted the repository's files. The safety check stopped it and nothing was deleted. New rule 14: never remove with a wildcard or variable path after `cd`; use a fresh `mktemp -d` folder.
- **A worked-out-in-my-head expectation again** (rule 12): the stall test's "still held" check came one step too late. I wrote the arithmetic out and fixed the test; the engine was right.
- **My e2e test inserted the poster picture onto the slide** (Insert → Picture…) and covered the video. It now uses Import Media…, which is what an author would do.
- **A locator matched six elements:** `getByLabel('Poster')` also caught the "poster" picture's row buttons. It now uses an exact match.
- **Double toggling and lost presses** came from my first board design: I trusted media events for the learner's actions. Recorded in pitfall #88.
