## Luke (2026-11-23), testing alpha 1 on Windows

He asked for the File / Edit / … menus to go behind a hamburger, on the same row as Save and Undo, with the relevant commands as icons and minimise, maximise and close at the top right of that row. He chose:

- **the compact layout:** the hamburger, Save, Undo, Redo and Preview; the title; Publish and the views; then the window's buttons;
- **macOS's conventions there:** the traffic lights at the left of our bar, and the menu in the macOS menu bar, with the hamburger as well.

See `docs/briefs/B1-title-bar.md`.

## What was done

- **The window has no title bar of its own** (`main/title-bar.ts`):
  - On Windows and Linux, the system draws its own minimise, maximise and close over the right end of our bar (`titleBarOverlay`, 40 px). They keep native behaviour, Windows 11's snap layouts included. Their colours follow the editor's theme: the bar sends its colours when the theme changes (`app:setTitleBarColours`).
  - On macOS, the traffic lights sit in our bar.
  - The bar keeps clear of them through the window-controls CSS variables.
- **`TitleBar.tsx`,** at the top of the window with or without a project:
  - the hamburger, which opens the whole application menu below itself (`app:showMenu`, `Menu.popup`);
  - Save, Undo, Redo and Preview;
  - the title, Publish and the views.
  - It is the window's handle: drag to move, double-click to maximise. Its buttons and the title field are not.
- **New icons in our own style:** the menu, Preview (a play triangle), and Master view, which had shared the slide's icon.

## Found on the way: the menu's shortcuts had stopped

**What happened:** with the title bar hidden, Windows (and Linux) show no menu bar and send no keys to its shortcuts. Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+M and the rest did nothing.

**How it showed:** the alpha package, built before this change, added a slide on Ctrl+M sent through Electron's input path. The new build did not.

**The fix:** main matches each key press against the menu's items before the page sees it, and runs the item (`main/accelerators.ts`). Letters and digits are matched by their key's place, so other keyboard layouts work. macOS is untouched (pitfall #159).

## Found on the way: the Linux e2e all failed (pitfall #160)

**What happened:** every Electron e2e in WSL (171 tests) failed at its first window. The app crashed (SIGSEGV) at start.

**The cause:** a bare Electron 45 script showed it. A window with `titleBarOverlay` crashes under `--ozone-platform=headless`, which is how the local Linux e2e keeps windows off the screen. Under xvfb (X11), the same window is fine.

**The fix:** with no screen, the window gets no overlay (`drawsOverlay`). On Linux, `title-bar.spec.ts` checks the overlay only under a real display, CI's xvfb included.

## Luke testing the alpha: video (same session)

He imported an mp4 and found two problems:

- it showed in Media as "a webm and an image";
- the video did not play when previewed.

He then made it clear that the playing problem was the timeline's Play in the editor, and asked for Space to play the timeline. The fixes:

- **Preview** (pitfall #161): the preview scheme served media whole, without `stream` or byte ranges. A longer video then stopped partway with a format error (`MEDIA_ERR_SRC_NOT_SUPPORTED`). It is now streamed, with 206 answers to `Range`. `e2e/preview-video.spec.ts` plays a 3 s 1280×720 VP9 to its end. It failed (error 4) on the alpha package and passes on the fix. On Windows and macOS, it also plays the H.264 fixture.
- **The editor's timeline** (pitfall #162): the video's sound played, but its picture on the stage never moved. `useTimelinePictures` now plays the stage's videos with the timeline, kept to the playhead, and shows the frame at the playhead when stopped or scrubbed.
- **Space:** pressed and let go, Space plays or pauses the timeline. Held while dragging, it still pans the stage. In fields and on buttons, it does what it always did.
- **Media:** an mp4 becomes a WebM and its poster picture. The poster is now the video's thumbnail in the list rather than an item of its own (`renderer/media-posters.ts`). `video.spec.ts` was changed to match: it had expected the poster as a second item.

`e2e/timeline-video.spec.ts` covers the following:

- Play moves the stage's video and Pause stops it.
- Space plays and pauses.
- Space held with a drag pans without playing.
- A space typed in the title goes into the title.
