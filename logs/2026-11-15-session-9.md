**Step 2 — precision and arranging** (commits `83b3e08`, `f34dd7f`, `8a71e2d`, `cb3e258`)

- Group / Ungroup from Home → Arrange and Ctrl+G / Ctrl+Shift+G, with the group selected afterwards and the freed children selected on ungroup.
- Lock, and Unlock All from Home → Arrange. Locked objects are skipped by clicks and the marquee, and cannot be moved, resized, rotated or deleted.
- Z-order: Bring to Front, Bring Forward, Send Backward, Send to Back (Ctrl+] / Ctrl+[ and Shift variants), keeping the selection's relative order.
- Align (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom) and distribute (horizontal, vertical), from upright bounds: one object aligns to the stage, several to the box around them; distribute leaves the outer two fixed.
- Snapping to other objects, the stage edges and centre, guides and grid lines, within 6 screen pixels. Ctrl/Cmd suspends it. Snap lines are drawn in the overlay.
- Rulers, grid and draggable guides, plus the View tab toggles. Drag a guide from a ruler, drag it to move, drag it back to a ruler to delete. Guides and the four toggles live in application settings (`settings.view`), so the saved project format is unchanged; Luke agreed to this in session 8.

**Step 3 — clipboard and menus** (commit `427e659`)

- `clipboard:read` / `clipboard:write` IPC channels using Electron's clipboard in main, added to `shared/ipc.ts`, `main/ipc.ts` and the preload bridge.
- `stage/clipboard.ts`: serialises `{ "vedraopus": 1, "objects": [...] }`, validates with `sceneObjectSchema`, gives every pasted object and group child a new id, shifts by 16 stage units on the slide it was copied from, and drops pictures whose file the receiving project lacks (re-boxing a group around what remains). Unit tested in `clipboard.test.ts`.
- Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate (Ctrl+D, without touching the clipboard) in `stage/actions.ts`. Copy records its source slide in renderer memory for the same-slide offset; a pending write is awaited so Paste straight after Copy works.
- Edit menu items are ordinary menu commands again, and `App.tsx` hands Cut/Copy/Paste to the browser when a text field has focus. Paste in a field reads through IPC and calls `document.execCommand('insertText')`, because the page may not run the clipboard's paste command.
- Right-click menu on the stage (`stage/ContextMenu.tsx`): selects what is under the pointer when it is outside the selection, then offers Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete, the four z-order commands, Group, Ungroup and Lock. Rendered in the page so it can be tested; not a native menu.
