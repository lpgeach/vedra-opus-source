The plan line asks for "scaffolding (string catalogue; English only at first)". The brief (`docs/briefs/P10.4-ui-strings.md`, written first) settles what that means:

- the catalogue and its rules;
- the most-seen surfaces moved into it;
- a pseudo-locale to show the rest.

The editor holds about 550 strings in 40 components; moving them all at once is not asked for. This is the last package of Phase 10.

## What was done

- **`apps/desktop/src/shared/strings/`:** `en.ts` (keys dotted by place, `as const`), `t(key, values)`, `setLocale` and `locale`, `StringKey`, and `pseudo`. It is plain TypeScript, shared by main and the renderer.
- **The pseudo-locale `qps-ploc`,** made from English at run time: `⟦Sävé ·⟧`, accented and a third longer. Placeholders and menu access keys are kept. `VEDRAOPUS_UI_LOCALE=qps-ploc` turns it on.
  - Main sets its locale before the menu is built.
  - The renderer reads it from `app.getInfo` (`uiLocale`, new) before its first draw.
- **In the catalogue now (101 keys):**
  - the application menu;
  - the Welcome screen;
  - the top bar;
  - the ribbon's tabs, and the Home and Insert tabs' groups and commands;
  - the right panel's tabs.

  The words are the same English, so every existing test that finds them by name still does.
- The ribbon's tab list was a module-level constant. Its words are now looked up as the ribbon draws, since the language is set after modules load.
- **`docs/development.md`** has a new section, "Adding words to the editor": new or changed editor text goes through `t()`, and the rest moves as surfaces are touched.

## Tests

- **Unit** (`main/strings.test.ts`):
  - English and placeholders;
  - falling back to English;
  - pseudo words, with placeholders and access keys;
  - a scan of every desktop source file: every `t('…')` key is in the catalogue, and every catalogue key is used (template keys count by their fixed start).
- **e2e** (`ui-strings.spec.ts`):
  - in the pseudo-locale, the menu, the Welcome screen, the version line, the top bar, the ribbon's tabs and commands, and the right panel's tabs show pseudo words;
  - a project opens and a text box is inserted;
  - in English everything is as it was.
