# Session 41: Slide and layer transitions (P6.6)

**Date**: 2026-11-18
**Participant**: Luke + AI Assistant
**Branch**: `wp/p6.6-transitions`, from `master` after P6.5 (PR #45). No CI (until December 2026); `[skip ci]` on commits and merges. Linux only (Windows and macOS are checked in December).

## Decisions

- **No question for Luke this time.** Transitions only draw, so plan §5 is unchanged: the slide's timeline starts on entry, triggers run at once, and the slide left takes no input. This is written in the brief.
- **When they play:**
  - a slide's transition plays when the learner arrives from another slide, but not on the first slide or on resume;
  - a layer's plays when it is shown, and backwards when it is hidden.

## Done

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P6.6-transitions.md`, written first.
- **Model (saved format 10):**
  - `slide.properties.transition` and `layer.properties.transition`;
  - eight kinds (fade, push, cover, uncover, wipe, split, iris, zoom) with directions per kind, durations 0.1–5 s, and `createTransition`;
  - validation: only an overlay layer may have one (not a base, master or layout layer);
  - migration 9 → 10 and the `v9-two-slides` legacy fixture.
  - Editing goes through `slide.setProperties` and `layer.setProperties`.
- **Engine:**
  - `EngineView.transition` (kind, direction, duration, time, `fromSlideId`) and `LayerView.transition` (`in`/`out`);
  - both run on the engine clock, whatever the timelines do;
  - a layer being hidden stays in the view, not shown, drawn as it was, until its out transition ends, and takes no input;
  - turning round part way starts the other direction as far in as it had got;
  - leaving the slide clears everything.
- **Render:** `transition.ts`, with `transitionKeyframes` (incoming, outgoing, which is on top) and `layerTransitionKeyframes`.
- **Player:**
  - each slide is drawn in `.vo-player-slide`, with the theme background so a cover hides what is beneath;
  - the slide left is kept as `.vo-player-leaving`, its media paused, until the transition ends;
  - paused Web Animations take their time from the engine;
  - layer containers are now the stage's size, so clip and scale work; they take no clicks themselves, and a closing layer's objects take none either.
- **Editor:**
  - **Transitions ribbon tab:** the slide's kind, direction and duration (uncover's directions read "To the …"), Preview, and Apply to all slides (one undo step);
  - a second group for the layer being edited;
  - Master View says transitions belong to slides;
  - **Preview** draws a copy of the slide or layer over the stage and plays it once (`stage/transition-preview.ts`); React's drawing is not touched;
  - **Slide list:** a mark under the number of a slide that has a transition (`data-transition`).

## Mistakes this session, and what changed

- **Layer containers were 0×0.** A wipe or iris on a layer would have clipped everything away, and zoom would have grown from the corner. I found it when a browser assertion failed for a different reason. The containers are now the stage's size, and a test checks it.
- **Changing structure without finding its readers.** The golden e2e test read the stage's direct children. This is now rule 16 in "Mistakes I keep making": grep for the selectors before changing the DOM.
- **Too many expectations pinned to the current format version.** Two model tests compared exact current values. One now uses "at least"; the other lists the new field.
