## Found and fixed

- **Previewing one slide could make an invalid project (P8.1 to P8.4 against Phase 7's preview scope).** `scopeProject` kept menu entries and custom tabs' lightboxes for slides it left out, for a bank question too. A course with either could not be previewed slide by slide. It now clears them (`playerWithout`). The test fails without the fix.
- **Moving a slide into a question bank left it named as a course slide.**
  - The references left behind: other slides' jumps and lightboxes to it, branches, menu entries and tabs.
  - The project was then invalid, and would not open again.
  - `slide.move` from a scene into a bank now clears them as removal does, and keeps the slide's own triggers.
  - The jump and branch half went back to Phase 7; Phase 8 added the menu, tabs and lightboxes.
  - The test fails without the fix.
- **During a lightbox the menu marked the lightbox's slide,** not the slide under it as P8.4's brief says. Fixed and tested.
- **Opening a lightbox lost keyboard focus:** the button pressed was redrawn away. Focus now moves to the lightbox's Close, and back to the player when it closes. The test fails without the fix.
- **In the Preview window, course files did nothing.** The Resources tab's files and the Open a file action were silently denied: the window opened only web and email links.
  - It now opens the project's own copy of the file in the system's app, with `shell.openPath`.
  - Only for the preview's own `content/assets/<hash>.<ext>`, and only of the Resources tab's types (never HTML or SVG). `previewFile` is tested.
  - Nothing leaves the machine.
- **Looks:**
  - In the light scheme, the frame ran into a white slide with no line between them. It now has one.
  - The speed menu was a bright native control in the dark frame. It is now styled as a frame control.
  - On a phone, the bar's buttons squashed ("Resou", "Gloss") instead of scrolling. They now keep their width and the tab row scrolls, as P8.5's brief says.
  - The Player dialog overflowed sideways, hiding the Remove buttons and Add link. It is wider now, and its rows wrap.
  - The choice of a lightbox slide named slides only by title, and they are usually all "Untitled Slide". The custom tab's choice and the trigger wizard now give the slide's number.
