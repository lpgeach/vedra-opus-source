## What was done

- **Model, format 34:**
  - `panorama` objects:
    - an equirectangular picture;
    - free or guided;
    - where the view starts (yaw, pitch, zoom);
    - up to 50 spots, each a marker with a title and text, or a hotspot;
  - the trigger events `spotClicked` (object, spot) and `panoramaVisited` (object);
  - validation:
    - the asset is a picture;
    - spot ids are unique in the project;
    - the events name a 360° image and one of its own spots;
  - removing a spot removes its triggers; copying a slide renews spot ids;
  - `createPanoramaObject` and `createPanoramaSpot`;
  - three label keys (`panorama`, `spotPrevious`, `spotNext`);
  - migration 33 → 34 changes nothing; legacy fixture `v33-two-slides`.
- **Engine:** `openSpot` marks a spot visited and runs its triggers, and says "all visited" once. Visited spots are kept as the slide's `p` and resumed; the view lists them.
- **Render:**
  - in a course, a focusable view with its start, a canvas and a real button per spot, named by it;
  - in the editor and thumbnails, the whole picture flat with a dot at each spot (numbered in the editor).
- **Player (`panorama.ts`):**
  - a WebGL view of the sphere, or, where WebGL may not read the picture, a flat pan;
  - spots placed over the view (`projectSpot`, `projectFlat`);
  - turning by drag, arrow keys and wheel, and zooming with + and −;
  - a marker's label (Escape closes it);
  - guided Previous and Next, which glide to each spot (they jump with reduced motion) and open it.
- **Editor:**
  - Insert → **360° Image…**;
  - the Format panel's **360° image** section: exploring, the start, and each spot's name, kind, yaw, pitch, title and text, with up, down and remove, and Add spot;
  - double-clicking the flat picture adds a spot there;
  - the wizard's two events and their sentences.
