Luke asked to go through all of Phase 8 and then review it. This is the fourth package. Its brief is `docs/briefs/P8.4-lightbox.md`; the design choices I made are listed at its top, for him to change.

Plan §5's lightbox rule is implemented as written, not changed: the slide under the lightbox is untouched, the lightbox has its own timeline and layers, and variables are shared.

## What was done

- **Model, format 23:**
  - the actions `openLightbox { slide, controls }` and `closeLightbox`;
  - custom tabs' `lightbox: { slide, controls } | null`, the second kind the P8.2 brief left for this package;
  - validation: a lightbox is a scene slide and not a draw;
  - removing a slide removes the triggers that open it and frees a tab that opened it;
  - `opensAsLightbox`; `slide.setDraw` refuses such a slide;
  - the label `lightbox`;
  - migration 22 → 23; legacy fixture `v22-two-slides`.
- **Engine:**
  - `under` holds the frozen slide; `openLightbox` and `closeLightbox` work as actions and from the player;
  - one lightbox at a time;
  - `goTo` closes a lightbox first, so jumps leave from the slide under it;
  - Previous and Next move the lightbox only when it has controls;
  - `view.lightbox`; the slide index and the saved session are the slide under it.
- **Player:**
  - the slide under it, cloned when the lightbox opens, is drawn still and dimmed behind it;
  - the lightbox is drawn at 80% of the stage;
  - a "Lightbox" bar with Close (and Previous and Next with controls);
  - Escape closes it;
  - a custom tab can open one.
- **Editor:**
  - the trigger wizard has "Open a slide as a lightbox" (the slide, and "with Previous and Next") and "Close the lightbox";
  - each custom tab has "does": run the slide's triggers, or open a slide as a lightbox (with Previous and Next, or not).
