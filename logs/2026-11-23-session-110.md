## What was done

- **The setting:** `view.offSlide` (`show`, `dim` or `hide`; `dim` by default) is in the settings, with the lenient reading falling back to Dim.
- **View tab → Off the Slide:** Show, Dim and Hide, each with its own icon in our style.
- **The stage:** `.stage-off-slide` covers what is past the slide in the pasteboard's colour, at half strength or in full. It takes no pointer, so dimmed and hidden objects are still picked and moved.
- **`e2e/pixels.ts`:** reads the colour at a point from a one-pixel screenshot, with no new dependency (Node's zlib).

## Found: the title bar's e2e timed out now and then

**What happened:** it had failed once in each of three earlier runs and passed when run alone. Its trace shows every step passing, and then `app.close()` never returning.

**The cause:** the test's last step adds a slide (Ctrl+M), which leaves unsaved changes. Closing then asks whether to save, in a real dialog that nothing answered. The app stayed open until the test timed out.

**The fix:** the test answers Don't Save, as the other tests do. It is a test fault, not the app's, so the package stands. `title-bar.spec.ts` three times over: 6 passed on the package, and 6 passed on the dev build.

**Note:** the unanswered dialog may have shown on Luke's desktop briefly during those runs, until the test killed the app. Any e2e step that leaves changes needs `answerDialogs(app, { message: [DONT_SAVE] })`.
