## What was done

The brief (`docs/briefs/P11.1-pptx-import.md`, written first) splits P11.1 into four parts. This is part A.

- **The reader** (`apps/desktop/src/main/import/pptx/`), pure functions over the deck's parts. It reads XML with the model's `parseXml`, by local name.
  - **The course:** the stage from the slide size (9525 EMU a pixel), the theme's colours and Latin fonts, and the title from the deck's properties, else the file name.
  - **Slides:** in order, each titled by its title placeholder, with its notes.
  - **Text:**
    - paragraphs, runs, line breaks and fields;
    - bold, italic, underline and strikethrough; size, colour and font, with theme colours as references and theme fonts as `heading` and `body`;
    - alignment;
    - bulleted and numbered lists, nested by level.

    A placeholder takes its master's text style (sizes, colours and bullets by level), then its master's and layout's own list styles.
  - **Shapes:**
    - preset geometries mapped to ours, with flips. A rounded rectangle keeps its corner, and diamonds, polygons, stars and block arrows become polygons.
    - solid and gradient fills; lines with colour, width and dashes; the shape style's fill and line where the shape gives none.
    - text boxes as text.
    - anything else becomes a rectangle, reported.
  - **Lines and connectors:** as long as their diagonal, turned to its angle, with an arrow where they have one.
  - **Pictures:** imported into the project's media, with their crop, alt text (the description) and decoration. A picture type a course cannot show (EMF, WMF) becomes a reported placeholder.
  - **Tables:** column widths, row heights, the header row and column, cell words and fills. Merged cells are shown unmerged, reported.
  - **Groups:** placed through their child space.
  - **Placeholders without a position** take their layout's, else their master's.
  - **Left as labelled, reported placeholders for part D:** charts, SmartArt, embedded objects and media. Ink is left out, reported.
  - **Hidden slides** are shown, reported. A file that is not a presentation, or has no slides, is refused with a reason.
- **`importPowerPointFile`** reads the zip within limits (200 MB a part, 1 GB in all, 20 000 parts) and stores each picture once, by its hash.
- **In the app:**
  - File → Import PowerPoint…, and a button on the Welcome screen;
  - the IPC call `project.importPowerPoint`;
  - a new, unsaved project opens, and the **import report** shows what did not come across as it was, by slide, with Copy report.

  The words are in the string catalogue (P10.4).

## Tests

- **Unit** (`convert.test.ts`), on decks built by `test-deck.ts` in the shape PowerPoint writes. There is no copy of PowerPoint here, and nothing is taken from real decks. The tests cover:
  - the course's size, theme, fonts and title, and the refusals;
  - run and paragraph formatting;
  - a body placeholder's inherited position, size and bullets, with nested levels and `buNone`;
  - preset geometries, fills, gradients, lines and dashes, and a reported unknown shape;
  - a flipped, rotated polygon;
  - a diagonal arrow's length, centre and angle;
  - pictures with their crop, alt text and decoration, and a reported EMF;
  - a table;
  - a group's child space;
  - a chart placeholder;
  - notes, and a hidden slide.
- **e2e** (`pptx-import.spec.ts`):
  - a two-slide deck imported through File → Import PowerPoint…;
  - the report (the chart);
  - the project's title, unsaved, and the slides' titles;
  - the bold text, the picture with its alt text in the Format panel, and the notes;
  - the table, and the chart's placeholder;
  - a file that is not a presentation is refused, saying why.
