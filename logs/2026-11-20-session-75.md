Luke asked (session 67) to go through all of Phase 9 and then review it. This is the last package of the phase. Its brief is `docs/briefs/P9.11-screen-recording.md`; the design choices I made are listed at its top, for him to change.

**On the rules:** nothing leaves the machine. The recording is held in memory, then stored as a project asset, and the pictures of screens and windows are shown only in the dialog. No native module or third-party file was added. The editor's permission rule (microphone only) is unchanged: capture goes through Electron's display-media handler alone, and only for a source the author just chose.

## What was done

- **Main:**
  - `main/screen-capture.ts`:
    - lists screens and windows (`desktopCapturer`, small pictures);
    - keeps the author's choice from the last list;
    - gives it to the next capture from the editor's own page, once, and refuses everything else;
  - Preview windows always refuse;
  - `storeRecording` takes a kind: a screen recording goes through the media pipeline as a WebM video with a poster, at most 500 MB;
  - three new IPC calls: `screen:sources`, `screen:choose` and `project:storeScreenRecording`.
- **Editor:** Insert → **Screen Recording…**:
  - pick a screen or window (shown as pictures, in two groups);
  - optional narration from the microphone;
  - a 3-second count, then Record and Stop (it stops by itself at 10 minutes);
  - watch the take back, record again, discard, or insert it as a video (one undo step for its assets, then the video is put on the slide as imported videos are).
- No saved-format change.

## Found, and fixed

- **A narrow window scrolled the whole editor sideways.** The new Screen Recording… button made the Insert ribbon wider than WSL's 960-pixel test window. The ribbon's row did not scroll by itself, so the page was wider than the window, and Playwright's click on a command past the edge scrolled the page 512 pixels left. That moved the stage half out of the window, and 9 Linux e2e tests failed (the stage's, and the 360° image's double-click). An author with a narrow window had the same problem. Two fixes:
  - **The command row scrolls by itself:** sideways, by the wheel, by Tab, or by a command scrolling into view. It has no visible scrollbar, so the ribbon is the same height on every tab and the stage's size does not change from tab to tab.
  - **Its menus and the shape gallery open fixed to the window**, under their button (leftwards near the right edge), so the scrolling row does not cut them off, and they follow the button if the row scrolls.

  A first try closed the menus when the row scrolled. Playwright's own scroll to a button then closed the menu it had just opened, and 31 Windows e2e tests failed. The menus now follow their button instead.
- **The new `editor-shell.spec.ts` test** opens a menu at the far end of the Insert ribbon in a narrow window. It checks that the row scrolls, the menu is whole inside the window, the page has not scrolled, and the editor still starts at the window's edge. It fails without the fix.
