# Session 42: Zoom regions and the cue-point workflow (P6.7)

**Date**: 2026-11-18
**Participant**: Luke + AI Assistant
**Branch**: `wp/p6.7-zoom-cues`, from `master` after P6.6 (PR #46). No CI (until December 2026); `[skip ci]` on commits and merges. Linux only.

## Decisions

- **Zoom-out timing:** a zoom region zooms out over the last part of its bar, finishing as the bar ends. This mirrors Luke's decision on exit animations, so I did not ask again.
- **Plan §5 is unchanged:** zoom regions add behaviour for a new object type only.
- **"While audio plays" needed sound in the editor first.** The editor's timeline Play used to move only the playhead and play no sound. It now plays the layer's sound, which is what makes "add cue points while audio plays" possible.

## Done

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P6.7-zoom-and-cues.md`, written first.
- **Model (saved format 11):**
  - `zoomRegion` objects with `props.speed` (0–3 s), and `createZoomRegion`;
  - validation: not inside a group, and no animations;
  - `object.update` refuses animations on a zoom region with a proper command error. The property test found this gap.
  - Migration 10 → 11 changes nothing; the `v10-two-slides` legacy fixture.
- **Engine:** `camera.ts` (`fitView`, `viewAt`) and `EngineView.camera`:
  - a region zooms in over `speed`, holds, and zooms out to finish at its end;
  - with overlapping regions, the later one wins and hands back when it ends;
  - a short region shares its time equally between zooming in and out;
  - the topmost shown layer with a region decides.
- **Render:** a zoom region draws nothing in a course, and a dashed "Zoom" frame on the editor stage.
- **Player:** the camera is applied to the slide's container with `scale` and `translate`.
- **Editor:**
  - **Insert → Zoom Region:** half the stage's size, at the playhead, for 3 s.
  - **Format tab:** a Zoom speed field.
  - **Timeline:**
    - Play plays the layer's sounds, and videos' sound from their trim start (`timeline-sound.ts`), each in its bar, at its volume, and kept in step;
    - **C** adds a cue point at the playhead while it plays;
    - **Align to cue point** starts the selected objects at a cue's time, as one undo step;
    - the **+ Cue** button's tooltip mentions C.

## Mistakes this session, and what changed

- **The stage size assumed again.** My camera tests assumed a 960 × 540 stage, but `createEmptyProject` makes one 1280 × 720. The tests now set the stage, and pitfall #93 says so.
- **An expected value worked out in my head (rule 12).** I wrote "1.5 times" for halfway into a ×2 zoom. It is the *view* that is halfway (720 wide), so the scale is 960 / 720 = 1.33. I recomputed and fixed the test.
- **A ref assigned during render** (pitfall #25) in the C shortcut. Lint caught it; it now uses `useEffectEvent`.
- **Dynamic imports in tests.** I wrote two tests with `await import`, then replaced them with ordinary imports.
