## What was done

- **File → Import Slides…** asks for a project or template file and lists its scenes and slides with checkboxes, plus All and None.
  - The chosen slides are copied in after the current slide, as one step that Undo takes back.
  - A summary says what came, what was added with it, and what was left behind.
- **The model** (`prepareSlideImport`, the `slides.import` command):
  - copies with new ids throughout;
  - jumps between copied slides point at the copies;
  - triggers naming anything that did not come are dropped and listed; question branches to slides that did not come go on to the next slide;
  - result slides keep the included questions that came;
  - a draw slide brings its bank, as a new bank, with its locked questions;
  - masters are added unless the same master is already here unchanged; one changed here is added with new ids, and the copy uses its layout;
  - variables are matched by name and type, otherwise added (renamed on a clash, with `%Name%` in the copies following);
  - the media the copies use comes with them.
- **Main:** `project:openSlideSource` reads the chosen file's project without unpacking it. `project:copySlideMedia` copies only the named media files, and only from that file (`extractAssets`).
- **Found and fixed:** Duplicate (`cloneSlides`) left a duplicated result slide naming the original's Passed and Failed layers, and a copy's Tab order naming the original's objects. The first made the project invalid, so saving would fail. Every reference inside a copy now follows (pitfall #144).
