# Session 52 — 2026-11-19

## What this session was for

P7.4, freeform questions: convert any slide into a question. The kinds are pick one, pick many, drag and drop (with drop targets, snapping, return-to-start and the three drag states), text entry, hotspot and shortcut key. Worked on the Windows machine straight after session 51 (P7.3, merged in PR #56), with Linux checks in WSL.

There is no AI attribution in any commit or in the PR.

## Done

On `wp/p7.4-freeform`, in four commits: the brief, then steps A, B and C as `docs/briefs/P7.4-freeform-questions.md` lays them out.

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P7.4-freeform-questions.md`. I settled the design questions myself; none is on the "ask Luke first" list. Luke may want to look at:
  - **Graded only.** Freeform questions are graded; there are no freeform survey questions.
  - **Shortcut-key presses.** Every key press on a shortcut-key slide is an answer, apart from Tab, modifiers alone, and typing in a field.
  - **Hotspot regions stay visible.** They are the author's own objects.
  - **Targets hold any number of items.**
- **Format 16:**
  - `freePickOne`, `freePickMany`, `freeText`, `freeKey`, `freeDrag` and `freeHotspot`;
  - the 15 → 16 migration changes nothing;
  - legacy fixture `fixtures/legacy/v15-two-slides`.
- **Model:**
  - `freeformKinds`;
  - `QuestionObject.type` `'onStage'`, for any object but audio and zoom regions, which validation checks;
  - `convertToQuestion`, `freeformQuestion` and `withSelectedState`;
  - `keyComboText` and `NOT_A_SHORTCUT`;
  - `withoutObjects`: a deleted drag target becomes no item's target, and a deleted region leaves the question.
- **Engine:**
  - freeform picks use the ordinary selection rules;
  - text entry is marked as fill in the blank;
  - `engine.key(key, modifiers)` now returns whether a shortcut-key question took the press;
  - drag and drop:
    - placements are saved as `target|x|y` (`freePlace` reads them);
    - snap and free placement;
    - return to start or stay where let go;
    - `engine.carry` drives the Drag Over state;
    - Drop Correct and Drop Incorrect are worked out in `shownState` from where the items are;
  - the freeform hotspot marks the whole slide, and its mark is on `EngineView.mark`.
- **Player:**
  - passes the modifiers held with a key, and stops an answering press (`preventDefault`);
  - reports a carried item's position;
  - turns a click anywhere on the stage into a freeform hotspot mark, except a click on the question's Submit, allowing for the camera;
  - draws the slide-level mark.
- **Editor:**
  - Insert → **Convert Slide** (a new icon of our own) and `editor.convertSlide`, one undo step;
  - Form View's **Parts** row: the question text box, the Submit button, and **Stop Being a Question**;
  - answers editors for each kind (`FreeformAnswers.tsx`);
  - repeated object names are numbered in the lists ("Rectangle 2"), so every control's name is unique;
  - the shortcut-key field records a press. Because the app's menu shortcuts might take some combinations first, there is also a key-name field and modifier checkboxes.

## Found and left alone

- **Browser-reserved shortcuts.** Browsers keep some presses (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N), so a course cannot ask for them. Form View says so.
- **Overlapping drops.** Targets hold any number of items, and snapped items stack on top of each other. "One item per target" is listed as later work in the brief.
- **The P7.3 log's notes still stand:** unchecked branch targets on slide delete, Likert points with no accessible names, and long essays on SCORM 1.2.
