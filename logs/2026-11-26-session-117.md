## B17 (`wp/b17-ribbon-and-panels`)

- **The zoom bar:**
  - **The cause:** with the window zoomed out (Ctrl+minus), the stage's scrollbars keep their size on screen, so in the page's own units they grow wider than the bar's 12 px margin, and covered it. Nothing kept the bar above objects past the slide either.
  - **The fix:** the bar now sits clear of the scrollbars as measured, above everything on the stage.
  - **The test:** `e2e/zoom-bar.spec.ts` zooms the window to 0.6, then zooms the stage right out and right in. It failed on the old code (`inside: false`) and passes on the new.
- **The right of the window as two panels:**
  - Above: Triggers · Format · Check. Below: Slide Layers · Slide Properties. Slide Properties is no longer under the Format tab's placeholder.
  - The divider between them is dragged or moved with the arrow keys, remembered (`layout.rightSplit` in settings, 260 px by default, older settings files take the default) and put back by Reset Layout. Neither panel goes below 100 px.
  - Tests: `e2e/right-panels.spec.ts`; unit tests for `clampRightSplit` and the settings default. Ten e2e files renamed "Layers" to "Slide Layers", and the ones that reached slide properties through Format now use Slide Properties.
- **A test fixed, not weakened:** `e2e/stage.spec.ts`'s Space-drag pan compared `scrollLeft` exactly (1037.2000122 against 1037.1999511). It failed with master's code too: scroll offsets are fractional under display scaling and kept in single precision. It now compares to a hundredth of a pixel.
- **The tab row** (Luke approved the four ideas from the review):
  - Preview (a menu: This Slide, This Scene, Entire Project) and Publish at its right end on every tab. They left the title bar and the Home tab.
  - A button collapses the ribbon to its tab row, remembered (`layout.ribbonCollapsed`). Collapsed, a tab's commands open over the stage until one is used or the author clicks elsewhere.
  - The contextual tab's object-type tag waits: with Text Format's groups on Home, no contextual tab is left.
  - Tests reach Preview with `previewFrom` (`e2e/launch.ts`); 19 files changed to it.
- **The stage toolbar:** the slide's number and name, the layer being edited (and switches it), the slide's size, and the zoom (out, the level with a menu, in, Fit, 100%). It replaces the floating zoom bar, so the bar can no longer be covered. `e2e/zoom-bar.spec.ts` now checks the zoom lies above the stage's scrolling area.
- **On the stage:** the selection's name above its top left and its size below its bottom right (`SelectionTags`).
- **The ribbon in seven tabs:**
  - Home, Insert, Slides (new), Design, Transitions, Animations and View.
  - Text Format's groups are on Home, dimmed with nothing to format, and editing text opens Home.
  - The brief's "As built" says where the tabs differ from its table. Commands kept their names, so authors and tests find them.
  - Ten e2e files changed: commands that moved go to the Slides tab, and Text Format became Home.
- **New commands:**
  - Find and Replace…, with a list of matches to jump to, and Replace All as one undo step (`find-replace.ts`).
  - Symbol, Hyperlink (a link on text being edited, or a click trigger on an object) and Clear Formatting.
  - Caption: rounded, square, oval and cloud callouts. Their geometry moved from the Storyline importer into the model (`callouts.ts`), and the importer uses it from there; its tests are unchanged and pass.
  - Slide Layer, Trigger (opens the wizard on the Triggers tab), Duplicate Slide, Tab Order (opens Slide Properties) and Feedback Master.
  - The Animation Painter (the Format Painter's code, carrying entrance and exit).
  - Five ready-made themes of our own.
- **A slide's own background, format 41:**
  - `slide.properties.background` is a fill (colour, gradient or picture) or null; `hideLayoutObjects` leaves out the layout's and master's objects.
  - Migration 40 → 41, legacy fixture `v40-two-slides`, and validation that a background picture is a picture the project has.
  - Drawn by `renderInheritedLayers`, so the stage, the player, thumbnails and handouts all show it.
  - Design → Background chooses it; Hide Layout Objects is an on/off command.
- **Apply to All** was already on the Transitions tab ("Apply to all slides").
- **A compact ribbon that never runs off the side** (Luke: "I never want the ribbon to go off the side of the screen", after his screenshots of the tool we match and another one):
  - Main commands large, the rest small and stacked three high; the ribbon is 86 px tall, room for three rows and the group's name.
  - Groups fold into drop-downs from the right when a tab is wider than the window, and unfold as room comes back.
  - Measured on every tab: nothing folds at 1600 or 1280 px; at 960 px, the narrowest window, Home folds Arrange and Insert folds Interactive Objects.
  - The first try of unfolding never unfolded: `scrollWidth` is never less than the row's own width, so it could not show spare room. It now adds up the groups' widths.
  - `editor-shell.spec.ts`'s narrow-window test expected the ribbon to scroll; it now checks that nothing overflows and that a folded group's menu opens whole, which was its point (the page must not scroll sideways).
  - New Slide is back on Insert, first, as in the tool we match; two stage tests clicked it there.
