## The course

**Where it is:** `C:\Users\lukeg\Documents\Vedra Opus courses\UK Space Industry 2026\`, outside the repository:

- the project, `The UK Space Industry in 2026.vedraopus` (11.9 MB);
- the course published from the app as a web folder and as a SCORM 2004 zip (11 MB);
- `About this course.txt`.

**How it was made.** A script used the app's own code: the model's factories, validated by `parseProject`; `importAsset` with our ffmpeg and `vo-decode` for media; `makeSpeech` with SAPI for narration; and `packContainer`. The project was then opened in the app (with the e2e harness, so no window showed) to:

- look at every slide;
- run the Check tab ("No problems found");
- publish it as a web course and a SCORM 2004 zip.

The published course was then played in Chromium with no errors. The script and the screenshots are in the session's scratchpad and are not committed.

**What it has:** 6 parts and 23 slides, plus an L5 lightbox slide in an Extras scene.

- **Media:** narration (Microsoft Hazel, en-GB) with captions and notes; music; two videos (one muted, one with sound and controls).
- **On the slides:** images, a table, eight map markers and a zoom region, a 360° image with spots, a scrolling panel, icons from the library, and a slider bound to a variable.
- **Animation:** entrance effects, including by paragraph, motion paths, and transitions of every kind.
- **Interaction:**
  - layers, with custom and visited states, and triggers with conditions;
  - nine variables, including the learner's name from a text field;
  - drag and drop with `droppedOn`, `animationCompletes`, `panoramaVisited` and `controlLosesFocus` triggers.
- **Questions:** a survey and five graded questions (multiple choice, true/false, multiple response, drag matching, drag sequence), a bank of three with one drawn at random, and a result slide used for tracking (passed or failed).
- **Player:** dark look, Resources and Glossary tabs, a custom tab opening the lightbox, checkboxes.

**Media, all free.** Every item and its licence is on the course's credits slide.

- **NASA, public domain:** five photos, a Curiosity panorama (padded to 2:1 at 4096 × 2048), and two videos. Clips were cut from the app's own WebM conversions. The Earth video's sound was removed: it opens with a quoted line on screen, so its soundtrack may hold someone else's recorded speech.
- **Music:** Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, credited.

**Not used:**

- Wikimedia Commons refused the downloads with "429 Too Many Requests". Its policy wants contact details from automated clients, and none were sent.
- NASA videos credited to Rocket Lab were left out: they are not NASA's own work.

**Facts.** Checked with web searches in November 2026. The course says plainly:

- no orbital launch from the UK yet; RFA One now aims for 2027;
- Orbex ceased trading in February 2026;
- the UK Space Agency's move into DSIT was due from April 2026. No source confirmed it had happened, so it is worded as a plan.

## B3: found while checking the course

**What happened:** the Mars 360° view was black when the published course was opened from a folder.

**The cause:** the flat fallback reused a canvas WebGL had claimed (pitfall #165).

**The fix:** draw flat on a fresh copy of the canvas. See `docs/briefs/B3-panorama-flat.md`.

## Verified

- **`e2e/panoramas.spec.ts`, its new check:**
  - fails before the fix ("no 2D context") and passes with it, on Windows;
  - from the course's own copy, a probe found 0 lit pixels before the fix. After it, the picture is drawn (the canvas is tainted), and a screenshot shows the Martian ground.
- **Over HTTP** the view used WebGL and drew, both before and after.
- **Player suite in WSL on the branch:** 344 passed, 1 failed: the known WebKit-in-WSL case `video.spec.ts:234`.
