Per rule 13, the branch and the brief (`docs/briefs/P6.1-audio.md`) came before any code. The brief implements plan section 5's existing clock rule ("media is the time master") and defines what play, pause and stop do to audio on a timeline, which the plan left open. No section 5 rule was changed.

## Done

- **Model (format 6):**
  - `audio` objects: asset, length, volume, and start (with the timeline or by a trigger). `createAudioObject`.
  - Actions `playMedia`, `pauseMedia` and `stopMedia`; event `mediaCompletes`.
  - Validation: the asset must be audio, and media actions and the event must name an audio object. Removing a sound removes its triggers.
  - Migration 5 → 6 (nothing to change) and legacy fixture `fixtures/legacy/v5-two-slides`. The property test now adds sounds with triggers.
- **Engine:**
  - A media state per audio object (`follow`, `free`, `paused`, `stopped`), reset on slide entry and when a layer is shown.
  - Media pauses with its layer. `mediaCompletes` fires once.
  - The time master is the earliest-starting playing `follow` audio. Its reports move the playhead forward only, a stalled master holds the timeline, and layers with no master fall back to the clock.
  - `engine.mediaTime()` and `LayerView.media`.
- **Render:** an audio object draws a speaker marker when the context asks (`mediaMarkers`, the editor's stage only) and an empty box otherwise.
- **Player (`audio.ts`):**
  - One audio element per sound on the slide, in a hidden holder off the stage.
  - Each tick it reports times first, then matches the elements to the view.
  - A refused `play()` pauses the slide behind "Play the course with sound".
- **Desktop:**
  - Insert → Audio… and Media panel → Insert decode the sound in the renderer for its length and waveform. The object goes on the slide from 0 s for its whole length, with its marker in the top-left corner.
  - The waveform is drawn in the timeline bar.
  - Format panel: Volume, Starts, Length.
  - Trigger wizard: Play, Pause and Stop media, and "media completes", with their sentences.
  - Insert → Record Audio…: record, stop, listen, insert or discard; time and level meter. It uses `getUserMedia` and MediaRecorder (WebM/Opus), and stores the take through the new IPC `project:storeRecording`.
  - **Permissions** (`main/permissions.ts`): Electron used to grant everything. Now the editor gets the microphone only (audio only, with the macOS system prompt), and Preview gets no devices. `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` is in the mac build config.
  - Preview windows allow sound without a gesture.
  - The asset scheme is CORS-enabled, and the CSP allows `fetch` of it, so sounds can be decoded.
- **Tests:**
  - Engine `media.test.ts`: 16.
  - Model: validation, removal with undo, the property step.
  - Render: marker on and off, plus visual `audio-marker.png` and "draws nothing in a course".
  - Renderer: waveform maths and the trigger sentences.
  - Main: recording storage.
  - Player browser `audio.spec.ts`: 3, with a generated WAV.
  - e2e `audio.spec.ts`: 2.
    - Insert a WAV: its length and waveform, volume and start, a Play media trigger, then save and reopen. Published and played, it is silent until clicked, then plays at 50% volume.
    - Record from Chromium's fake microphone and insert the take.

## Found along the way, and fixed

- **"Failed to fetch" when decoding a sound.** The editor's page loads from `file://`, and fetching `vedraopus-asset://` is cross-origin: images and media elements worked, but `fetch` did not. Fixed with `corsEnabled` on the scheme, an allow-origin header, and `connect-src vedraopus-asset:` in the CSP (pitfall #85).
- **A mock-LRS test leak in Firefox.** This predates P6.1 (P5.4 and P5.5 specs). A course page left open at the end of one test sent its keepalive statements into the next test's LRS. Both specs now leave the page and wait for `terminated` in `afterEach` (pitfall #87). No check was loosened.

## Not done or not verified

- **Preview playing sound** without a gesture is configured (`autoplayPolicy`) but not tested.
- **The macOS microphone prompt** and its usage text are untested; there is no Mac here.
- **Seeking in recordings may be slow.** Chromium writes WebM recordings without a duration or cues. Our length comes from decoding, so timing is right. P6.3 will convert recordings to mp3.
- **No m4a, mp3 or ogg file** was imported in a test. They go through the same Web Audio decoding as WAV; only WAV was used.
- **The editor does not play sound while you scrub** (left out in the brief).
- **Media state is not saved for resume.** A `free` sound starts again in its start mode.
- **Not tested on:** WebKit, macOS, Windows, CI.

## Mistakes this session, and what changed

- **Two engine tests that proved nothing,** caught before committing. One asserted a list the test had filled itself. The other built a fake group with `as never` instead of `createGroupObject`. Both now check real behaviour. These are the "test that asserts nothing" kind that rule 10 covers.
- **I forgot `fetch` needs CORS on the custom scheme,** although images already used it. The e2e test caught it on its first run, and it is now recorded (pitfall #85).
- **The LRS test leak** was mine from P5.4; it only showed under load in Firefox. Recorded as pitfall #87, with the rule for any spec that shares a mock server.
