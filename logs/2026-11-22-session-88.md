## What was done

- **Masters:** each master the presentation lists becomes one of ours (`kind: 'slide'`), named as the deck names it. They go before the project's feedback master.
  - Our master's layer holds only the PowerPoint master's background.
- **Layouts:** each layout the master lists becomes one of ours, named as the deck names it. Its layer holds:
  - its own background;
  - the master's own shapes (read afresh for each layout, so each has its own copies), unless the layout hides them (`showMasterSp="0"`). That is how PowerPoint draws them.
  - its own shapes;
  - our title and content placeholders, where the layout puts them (or its master does). Dates, footers and slide numbers are not our placeholders and are left out.
- **Slides** use their layout (`layoutId`). A slide's title and body text boxes are marked as its copies of the layout's placeholders (`props.placeholder`).
  - A slide that hides its master's shapes is reported, since here they show through its layout.
- **Backgrounds:** a slide's, layout's or master's own background becomes a locked, decorative shape or picture the size of the stage, at the bottom of its layer. It can be a colour, a gradient, a picture, or the colour of a theme background reference.
- The converter now reads every part (slide, layout, master) through one context builder. A placeholder takes after the nearest of its layout and master, and its text defaults apply furthest first, so the nearest list style wins.
