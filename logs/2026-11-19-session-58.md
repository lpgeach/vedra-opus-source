## What was done

- **Model, format 21:** `project.player.tabs`:
  - Resources: links and files;
  - Glossary: terms;
  - Notes: shown or not;
  - custom tabs.

  Also:
  - the trigger event `playerTab`;
  - migration 20 → 21; legacy fixture `v20-two-slides`;
  - validation: link schemes, file assets, unique ids, tab triggers;
  - `project.setPlayer` deletes the triggers of a removed custom tab;
  - new `slide.setNotes` command; helpers `notesFromText` and `notesText`;
  - id prefixes `tab`, `res` and `term`.
- **Engine:** `engine.playerTab(tabId)` runs the current slide's `playerTab` triggers.
- **Player (`packages/player/src/tabs.ts`):**
  - tab buttons in the bar, in a "Course tabs" group;
  - one panel at a time, named after its tab;
  - a second click or Escape closes it, and focus returns to the tab;
  - Resources links open in a new tab (`noopener noreferrer`), and files carry `download`;
  - the glossary is sorted by term;
  - Notes follow the slide;
  - a custom tab fires its triggers;
  - a built-in tab with nothing in it is not shown.
- **Main:** the new IPC call `project:importFiles`. Its allow-list (`RESOURCE_TYPES`) is documents and pictures: never HTML, SVG, scripts or programs, because a resource opened from the course would run them.
- **Editor:**
  - the Player dialog's Tabs section:
    - Resources: Show, items with titles and Remove, Add link, Add file…;
    - Glossary: Show, terms and definitions, Remove, Add term;
    - Notes: Show;
    - Custom tabs: labels, Remove, Add tab;
  - the bottom panel's Notes tab is a real editor now, a paragraph a line, saved as one undo step on blur;
  - the trigger wizard has "the learner clicks the player tab".
