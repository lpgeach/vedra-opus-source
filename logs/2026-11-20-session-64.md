## Done so far (committed on the branch)

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P9.1-hotspots-markers.md` (commit `f2751c6`), with the design choices listed at the top for Luke. The marker's plain text is `props.body`, not `text`: other code treats any `props.text` as rich text.
- **Model, format 26:**
  - `hotspotObjectSchema` (`props.shape: 'rect' | 'ellipse'`) and `markerObjectSchema`, with `markerIcons` and the types `HotspotObject`, `MarkerObject` and `MarkerIcon`. Marker props:
    - icon and colour;
    - title and body;
    - media (a picture or video);
    - audio;
    - opens on click or hover;
    - pulse.
  - Both are in `SceneObject` and `sceneObjectSchema`.
  - Validation: a marker's media must be an image or video asset, its audio an audio asset.
  - `createHotspotObject(frame, shape, name)` and `createMarkerObject(frame, title, name)` (36 × 36, plus icon, colour `accent1`, opens on click).
  - Migration 25 → 26 changes nothing. Legacy fixture `fixtures/legacy/v25-two-slides`; current fixtures migrated to 26.
- **Render:**
  - `renderHotspot`: in a course, empty, with an ellipse clipped by `clip-path`. With `mediaMarkers` (the editor's stage), a dashed outline and a "Hotspot" word.
  - `renderMarker`: a circle in the colour, with a glyph from `MARKER_GLYPHS` on a 24-unit grid, in black or white by `textOnAccent`. `data-pulse="true"` when it pulses.
  - Unit tests in `packages/render/src/render.test.ts` ("hotspots and markers (P9.1)").
  - Two visual cases in `packages/render/visual/objects.spec.ts` ("markers" and "hotspot-marked"). **Their reference images do not exist yet** (see below).
- **Player (started, not wired in):** `packages/player/src/markers.ts`:
  - `labelPlace` (right of the marker, else left, kept inside the stage) and `bodyParagraphs`;
  - `createMarkers(document, project, stage, { assetUrl, level, rate })`, which returns `activate(id)`, `close()`, `isPressable(id)`, `mark(drawn, visible)` and `destroy()`.
  - It builds the label (a title, paragraphs, a picture or video, a sound at the learner's volume and speed), opens hover markers on pointer and focus, closes on a press elsewhere, and gives hotspots and markers button semantics. It is not yet used by `player.ts`, and has no tests.
  - It has a needless helper, `readStageSize` (the expression `stage.ownerDocument.defaultView ? readStageSize() : project.stage`). Replace it with `project.stage`.
