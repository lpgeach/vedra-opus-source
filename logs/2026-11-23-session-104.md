They are: Import PowerPoint, New from Template, a template's replace and delete questions, Import Slides, the file-could-not-be-read messages, Export Handout and Export Video, the crash-report and update messages. 34 keys added.      - **The starter templates failed the accessibility checker** (P10.2). Their buttons' words were 2.3 : 1 against the first accent (4.5 : 1 needed), because the palettes' dark first accent fills buttons whose words are the dark text. The first accents are lighter shades of the same hue now (harbour `#5b9bd5`, about 5.5 : 1; meadow `#6aaa64`, about 5.4 : 1). A test runs `accessibilityIssues` on every starter template and expects nothing. This is separate from the waiting question about the default theme's buttons (3.6 : 1, session 81).
     - **The handout ignored the course's language** (P10.3). An Arabic course's handout was laid out left to right.

       - The picture descriptions no longer have the English word "Slide": they read "3. Title".
       - Tested by unit tests (English and Arabic documents) and by the phase's e2e.
