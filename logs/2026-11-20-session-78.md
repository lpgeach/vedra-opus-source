## Found before changing anything

axe-core found only one problem on the fixtures and an all-types course: a 360° spot's name did not contain the number it shows. But axe cannot see these, which are worse:

- **A course's own buttons, and objects with click triggers, could not be reached or pressed from the keyboard.** Only hotspots, markers and choices could.
- **"Visible to assistive technology" off was ignored,** except by graphics and tables. Decoration was read out.
- **The course was `role="application"`,** which turns off a screen reader's reading keys for all of it.
- **There was no skip link, and no announcement of slide changes, layers or results.**
- **Slide objects had no focus style.**

## What was done

- **`packages/player/src/semantics.ts`**, applied after every draw:
  - buttons, and anything a click trigger names, are buttons, named, reached with Tab, pressed with Space or Enter, and disabled when their state is;
  - decoration is hidden;
  - a wordless shape with alt text is a named picture;
  - a group with alt text is a named group.
- **The course is a named region.** The slide is a region named by its title, and the controls are a named group.
- **"Skip to slide"** is the first Tab stop, and moves focus to the slide's first object. There are two new label keys: `skipToSlide` and `courseControls`.
- **A polite live region** says each new slide's title, a layer shown (feedback layers included) and a question's result.
- **Visible focus:** a white ring inside a dark one on slide objects, so it shows on light and dark slides.
- **A 360° spot's name** starts with its number ("1: Door"), as WCAG 2.5.3 asks.
- **`packages/player/browser/a11y.spec.ts`** runs axe-core on every slide of every fixture project and of a course with every kind of object, in Chromium and Firefox. It also covers:
  - Tab reaching a slide button and Enter pressing it;
  - the skip link;
  - decoration hidden and named pictures;
  - the focus ring;
  - the announcements of a new slide and of a question's result.

## Found while testing, and fixed

- **Focus fell to the page when a button changed the slide.**
  - A slide button now takes focus when pressed. When its slide went, focus fell to the page body, where the course's keys no longer reach: a freeform shortcut-key question could not be answered after pressing Submit. A keyboard learner would have met it too.
  - Now, when focus was on the slide that goes, it moves to the new slide's region.
- **A freeform question's choices were hidden.** Shapes start decorative, so the first version hid a freeform question's choice shapes, which are radio buttons.
  - Now anything a question answers with, and anything draggable, keeps its own semantics.
  - The all-types course now has a freeform question, so axe-core would see it.
