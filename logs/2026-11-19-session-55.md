- a result is worked out on arrival at its slide;
- unanswered questions score 0;

- answers are named by letter in position order;
- a Likert question is one interaction per statement.

## Done

### Step A: results (format 19)

- **Model:**
  - `slide.result`: kind, include, pass mark, timer, and the Passed and Failed layers;
  - tracking `completion: { kind: 'result', slide }`;
  - the actions `exitReview`, `retryQuiz` and `printResults`.
- **Validation:**
  - a slide has at most one role: question, draw or result;
  - a result cannot include itself, and cycles are refused;
  - included slides must be question, draw or result slides;
  - tracking must name a graded result.
- **Commands:**
  - `slide.setResult`;
  - deleting slides or layers updates results, and tracking falls back to "trigger";
  - `slide.setQuestion` and `slide.setDraw` refuse result slides (the property test found that gap).
- **Migration 18 → 19,** with the legacy fixture `v18-two-slides`.
- **Engine:**
  - `resultQuestions` follows draws and nested results, each question once;
  - scoring and pass/fail;
  - survey counts;
  - the Passed and Failed layers;
  - the `Result*` built-ins;
  - tracking success and score, saved in `SessionState.res`.
- **Codec:** `res` and `tm` (rule 17). When space runs out they are dropped together with the answers, last.
- **LMS:** `setScore` is called before `setStatus`.
- **Editor:**
  - Insert → Question → Result Slide;
  - the Result form;
  - the Tracking dialog's result choice;
  - the new actions in the trigger wizard.

### Step B: review, retry, print and the timer

- **Review from a result slide** covers its questions only. Next and Previous move among them, and Next past the last goes back to the result. `exitReview` leaves at any time.
- **Retry** redraws, rebuilds the course order, clears the result's questions, their slides' memory, the result and the timer, then goes to the first question.
- **Print:** a `ResultSummary` that the player prints from a new window, or hands to `onPrintResults`.
- **The timer:**
  - starts at the first question;
  - counts down in `ResultTimeLeft` and in the player's bar;
  - at 0, goes to the result slide;
  - is kept on resume.

### Step C: interactions

- **`interactionsOf`:** every kind's type, response and correct pattern as `InteractionAnswer` parts, and the result.
- **`formatAnswer`:** SCORM 1.2's form, or SCORM 2004's, which xAPI shares.
- **Driver changes:**
  - the SCORM 1.2 driver records `long-fill-in` as `fill-in`;
  - all three drivers write the parts.
- **The LMS start code** reports each finished question, with its latency.

## Tests changed, and why

- **The drivers' interaction tests** (`scorm12.test.ts`, `scorm2004.test.ts`, `xapi.test.ts`) now pass parts instead of strings. The interface changed by design; the written values are the same.
- **A wrong expectation in my own timer test:** `ResultTimeLeft` is 0 away from a result slide when nothing is tracked, as the design says.
- **My retry test** no longer assumes which question a random draw picks.
