# Session 46: Question model and Form View (P7.1)

**Date**: 2026-11-18
**Participant**: Luke + AI Assistant
**Branch**: `wp/p7.1-question-model`, from `master` after the reduced-motion follow-up (PR #50). No CI (until December 2026); `[skip ci]`. Linux only.

The first package of Phase 7, Quizzing.

## Luke's decisions

Asked before the brief, because they shape the whole phase:

- **Linked objects:** the form makes ordinary objects on the slide (the prompt, a choice object per answer, Submit), which Slide View moves and styles.
- **First types:** multiple choice and multiple response; P7.2 adds the rest.
- **Answer and submit in the course:** Submit checks, scores and branches. Feedback layers, try-again and review stay in P7.5.

## Done

- **Brief:** `docs/briefs/P7.1-question-model.md`, written first.
- **Model (saved format 13):**
  - `slide.question`: kind, prompt, submit and choices (each with correct, points and feedback), scoring by question or by choice, attempts, shuffle, feedback (by question, by choice or none, with texts), branching after correct and after incorrect, and required;
  - the new `choice` object type (a radio or checkbox mark beside its words) and the `submitInteraction` action;
  - validation: the parts are of the right type, on the slide's base layer, each used once; multiple choice has at most one correct answer; choice ids are unique;
  - `slide.setQuestion`; `object.remove` takes deleted parts out of their question; copies get new choice ids;
  - `createChoiceObject` and `createQuestionSlide`;
  - migration 12 → 13 and the `v12-two-slides` legacy fixture.
- **Engine:** `quiz.ts` (`markAnswer`, `bestScore`, `shuffled`) and `submitQuestion`:
  - correct and incorrect for both kinds, with the score by question or by choice, floored at 0;
  - attempts and try-again;
  - the branch, or the next slide by default;
  - an unanswered Submit raises `questionUnanswered` and uses no attempt;
  - a required question holds Next (`canNext`, `next()` and auto-advance);
  - a finished question is locked, and shows its answer on revisit;
  - an unfinished one starts again on a fresh visit but not when resumed;
  - shuffling, with an injectable `random`, is drawn through the offsets;
  - answers are kept in `SessionState.q`.
- **Suspend codec:** carries `q` (rule 17), and drops it last when the data must be squeezed.
- **Render:** a choice's mark; `renderObject` takes the state it draws in.
- **Player** and **editor stage:** they pass that state.
- **Editor:**
  - **Insert → Multiple Choice / Multiple Response:** adds a question slide after the current one and opens its form.
  - **The Form/Slide switch** above a question slide.
  - **Form View** (`QuestionForm.tsx`):
    - type, question text, and answers (words, correct, points by choice, feedback by choice, move up and down, delete, add);
    - scoring, attempts, shuffle and required;
    - feedback mode and texts;
    - the two branches;
    - a warning when no answer is correct.
  - **Edits** go through `question-edit.ts`, one undo step each.
  - **Elsewhere:** a "?" mark in the slide list, and "Submit the question" in the trigger wizard.

## Found on the way

- **Visual tests and text.** `objects.spec.ts` deliberately has no text, because fonts differ between systems. The new choices test, and the zoom-region test from the Phase 6 review, had words in them; the browsers disagreed on the choices. Both now hide their text, and the words are checked in unit tests.
- **Duplicate names in Form View.** The "Attempts" and "Feedback" groups and their selects had the same names. The selects are now "Attempts allowed" and "Feedback by".
