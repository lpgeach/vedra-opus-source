## What was done

- **Export Word…** in the Export Handout dialog writes a `.docx` with the same choices as the PDF:
  - one slide a page with its heading, picture and notes;
  - or three a page, each picture beside lines for the reader's notes.
- **Pictures:** main draws each slide's markup alone in one hidden, offscreen window with scripts off, and captures it. Nothing is shown.
  - The first try asked for a window twice the slide's size. It was held to the screen (1440 × 852 here), and the pictures were cut off.
  - Now each slide is zoomed to fit the window it is given, up to twice its size, keeping its shape.
- **The document:**
  - our own small `.docx` writer: title and heading styles (an outline), inline pictures with descriptions for screen readers, notes as paragraphs (bullets and numbers kept as text), A4;
  - it needs no other program.
- **Checked by eye:**
  - the pictures: the whole slide, its own picture loaded through the app's media scheme;
  - the full LibreOffice Writer (from the trim study) opened the document and printed it to a two-page PDF with title, headings and framed pictures.

  The trimmed LibreOffice the app ships cannot open Word files (no Writer), as intended.

## Tests

- **Unit:**
  - `handout-document.test.ts`: the parts, well formed; pictures, descriptions, headings, escaping, notes; one page each; the three-a-page tables and their page breaks;
  - `handout.test.ts`: notes as plain paragraphs.
- **e2e** (`handout.spec.ts`): Export Word… writes a `.docx` whose document names both slides, with a PNG of each in the slide's shape, at least 1000 pixels wide, and not blank.
