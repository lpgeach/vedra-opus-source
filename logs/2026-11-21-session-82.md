## What was done

- **`translationUnits(project)`** (`packages/model/src/translation.ts`): every piece of text a learner can meet, each with a stable id, its words and a note for the translator. That covers:
  - the course, scene and slide titles;
  - slide notes, and overlay layer names;
  - every object's text, by paragraph and list item;
  - states' own text;
  - table cells;
  - alt text;
  - field placeholders and dropdown options;
  - markers' titles and labels;
  - 360° spots, web object titles and captions;
  - player menu entry titles;
  - resources, glossary and custom tabs;
  - every player word.

  It goes through masters, layouts and question banks too.
- **Formatting** goes out as numbered tags, one number per distinct set of marks, and line breaks as breaks. `%Name%` stays as written.
- **`applyTranslation`** puts targets back, rebuilding the marks from the numbers, and makes the target language the course's. The player words are saved for it.
  - A unit is skipped and counted when it:
    - changed in the project after export;
    - is no longer in the project;
    - was left untranslated;
    - is unusable (unknown tags, or a length its field does not allow).
  - It is the `project.translate` command: one undo step.
- **XLIFF 1.2** (`packages/model/src/xliff.ts`):
  - one `<file>` with its languages, and a `<trans-unit>` per unit;
  - formatting as `<g id="n">` and line breaks as `<x id="br"/>`.
  - Reading uses the model's own small XML reader. It handles entities, CDATA, comments, groups and `<mrk>`, and it refuses anything else, saying why.
- **The Translation dialog:** File → Translation…, or Design → Translation… beside Player…:
  - the course's language, and a language to translate into;
  - Export XLIFF…;
  - Import translation…, which says what it did.

  Two IPC calls, `project.saveTranslation` and `project.openTranslation`, follow the captions calls.
- A globe icon of our own for the command.

## Tests

- **Model** (`translation.test.ts`):
  - every kind of unit;
  - formatting tags, breaks and `%Name%`;
  - applying every unit, with formatting kept on the translated words;
  - the player words and language;
  - changed, missing, untranslated and invalid units counted;
  - one undo step, and a bad language tag refused.
  - **Round trips over every fixture:** an untranslated file, and the source given as the translation, both leave the project exactly as it was.
  - **XLIFF:** writing, reading back, tools' forms (entities, CDATA, groups, `<mrk>`), and the refusals.
- **e2e** (`translation.spec.ts`):
  - a new course with a button: the course's own language refused as the target;
  - exported to French, every target filled, then imported: all translated;
  - the slide and the title show the French;
  - published: the player is `lang="fr"`, with the French Next, and the slide's French;
  - undo brings the English back.
