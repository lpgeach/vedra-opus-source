## The run-through

A throwaway Playwright script, not committed, did the following:

- published the course from the app;
- played it in Chromium at 2× from `file://`, slide by slide;
- took pictures at 0.7 s, at 2.3 s, and once each slide's timeline had played;
- tried every interaction:
  - the name, map markers, spaceports, loading and launching, the launch video;
  - the history slider, the astronaut cards, the 360° spots;
  - the scrolling panel, the L5 lightbox, every question answered right, the checklist, the tabs.

It was run twice, with 103 pictures each time, looked at in sheets. No player errors or warnings either time.

## B4: the demo course in the app

See `docs/briefs/B4-demo-course.md`.

- **Where it lives:** the course is in `apps/desktop/resources/templates/` (11.4 MB), and the installer copies that folder (`electron-builder.yml`). It is therefore also a built-in template.
- **How it opens:** the Welcome screen's **Explore the Demo Course** and **Help → Open Demo Course** open a new, unsaved copy (`template:openDemo`). A build without the file says so.
- **Credits:** Help → Third-Party Notices… has "Demo course media", which credits the music (CC BY 4.0) and NASA. The same credit is in `docs/licences.md`.
- **Version:** `0.1.0-alpha.3`.
- **Also fixed:** `e2e/off-slide.spec.ts` was not formatted on `master` (from B2).
