## What was done

- **Slide transitions** (`motion.ts`, `readTransition`) become ours:
  - fade, push, cover, pull (as uncover), wipe, split (horizontal or vertical), circle (as iris) and zoom;
  - PowerPoint's direction of movement becomes ours, which is where the new slide comes from;
  - a newer transition's exact duration (`p14:dur`), else its speed;
  - one we lack: the deck's own fallback, else a fade, reported. A diagonal Cover runs straight, reported.
- **Animations** (`applyAnimations`): the main sequence on the slide's timeline, as if every click came at once:
  - a click starts after everything before it; "with previous" starts with it; "after previous" starts at its end; delays are added;
  - entrances (Fly In with its direction, Fade, Split, Wipe, Circle, Zoom, Bounce; Appear as timing only) set when the object appears;
  - exits set when it leaves;
  - motion paths become ours (a line, or a freeform keeping its corners and curve ends);
  - the timeline grows to hold it all.
  - Reported: emphasis effects; effects we lack (shown as a fade); two ids taken as Float and Wheel as Spin, whose mapping could not be checked against PowerPoint here; animations of things inside groups; and, once per slide, that click-started animations play in order.
- **Sound and video:** a picture-frame media object (with PowerPoint 2010's embedded `p14:media`, or an older link inside the deck) is left as a stand-in. Then `importPowerPointFile` runs its file through the media pipeline (`importAsset`) and puts a video or sound object in the stand-in's place, keeping its frame, name, start and alt text.
  - Without the media tools, or for a format courses do not use, the stand-in stays and the report says why.

## Tests

  - the timeline: click, with, after with a delay, and a later exit, with each object's start, duration and effects, and a valid project;
  - motion paths: a line and a curve's ends;
  - the timeline growing;
  - reported emphasis and an unknown effect.
- **Unit** (`import-file.test.ts`): a deck with a video and a sound.
  - Without media tools they stay reported placeholders.
  - With the real ffmpeg (made with ffmpeg's own test sources), they become a WebM video and an MP3 sound in place, with nothing reported. It ran on Windows; it is skipped where there is no ffmpeg.
