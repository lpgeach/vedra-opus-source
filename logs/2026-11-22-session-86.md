## The review

1. **Against the plan and the briefs.** Every item of P10.1's plan line is in the course: semantic roles, alt text, the focus-order editor, visible focus, keyboard operation with dragging, skip navigation, announcements, reduced motion (from Phase 6), learner-adjustable text, and contrast. P10.2 to P10.4 each do what their briefs say. What the packages had not checked *together*:
   - **The Tab order list named objects by their object name**, so two buttons the author had named "Left" and "Right" by alt text both showed as "Button". P10.1 part B came before P10.2's `courseName`. The list and its Move buttons now use the name the learner hears. The Phase 10 e2e found this.
   - **The text view's lines did not take their own direction,** though slide paragraphs did after P10.3. English lines in an Arabic course would align right. Each line is now `dir="auto"`.
2. **Rules.** Plan §5 (triggers and runtime) was not touched by Phase 10. The model's invariants gained one for translation (`skills/model-package.md`): it changes words only, never ids or structure.
3. **Undo and redo.** `project.translate` was missing from the random undo/redo property test. It is in now, as a `translate` step that translates every other unit into French, Arabic or German. P10.1's `focusOrder` step was already there.
4. **End to end** (`e2e/phase10-course.spec.ts`), through the UI only:
   - a picture and two buttons named by alt text in the Format panel;
   - the author's Tab order;
   - the Check tab showing only the default buttons' contrast;
   - a pale accent on the light scheme, and the Player dialog's note;
   - saved, reopened, and all of it still there;
   - exported as XLIFF, translated into Arabic and imported;
   - Preview right to left, in Arabic;
   - published: `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"`, the translated alt texts naming the picture and the buttons, the translated skip link going to the author's first stop, Tab to the second, the strengthened accent, and the text view in Arabic.
5. **Looks.** 18 screenshots, taken headless in WSL by a throwaway spec (not committed), and looked at:
   - the Format panel's Accessibility section, the Check tab, the Tab order, the Player dialog and the Translation dialog, in the editor's light and dark themes;
   - the pseudo-locale editor and Welcome screen;
   - published courses in English and Arabic: the accessibility panel, the text view, and 320 pixels wide.

   They found three more things, fixed and seen again after the fix:
   - **The accessibility panel's settings ran together on one line** ("Text viewText size"). Each is now its own row. The panel also sits at the end of the line (`inset-inline-end`), so it is on the left in a right-to-left course, and its list indents at the start.
   - **The Translation dialog's four buttons wrapped onto two lines each.** The dialog is wider, and the buttons keep to one line.
   - **The pseudo-locale wrapped the top bar's buttons** ("Publish", the views), which is what longer translated words would do. They keep to one line.
6. **Full checks:** see Verification.
