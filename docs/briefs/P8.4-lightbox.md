# P8.4 Lightbox slides

**Work package:** P8.4. It needs P8.1 to P8.3.

**Plan line:** `open lightbox` / `close lightbox` actions per section 5, with optional navigation controls.

**Plan §5, unchanged:** "Opens another slide in an overlay over the current one with its own engine scope for timeline and layers; variables are shared; closing returns to the untouched underlying slide."

**My design choices** (Luke asked to go through all of Phase 8), listed for him to change:

1. **What "untouched" means:**
   - while a lightbox is open, the slide under it is frozen: its timeline, media, states and layers stay as they were, and none of its triggers run;
   - closing gives it back exactly so, with no `timeline starts` again.
2. **The lightbox slide is any slide of the course's scenes**, except a draw slide (it stands for other slides) and the slide under it.
   - It plays as itself: its own timeline from 0, or resumed per its revisit setting, with its layers, questions and triggers.
   - It counts as seen, for menu navigation and tracking.
3. **Opening a lightbox while one is open** replaces it. Only one is open at a time.
4. **A jump from inside a lightbox** (a trigger, the menu, a branch) closes it first, then jumps from the slide under it. "Next" and "previous" in a jump are therefore relative to the slide under it.
5. **Navigation controls are optional, per action.**
   - With them on, Previous and Next move the lightbox to the slide before or after it in the course's order, staying a lightbox. The lightbox's own bar shows them, and the player's Previous and Next (and the arrow keys) do the same.
   - With them off, there are no Previous or Next: only Close.

   Close and Escape always close the lightbox.

6. **The slide count and the menu** go on showing the slide under the lightbox.
7. **A resumed session** comes back to the slide under the lightbox, with the lightbox closed.
8. **Custom tabs (from P8.2)** can now open a slide as a lightbox, the second kind the P8.2 brief left for this package. The other kind still runs the slide's triggers.

## Saved format version 23

- **Actions:**
  - `{ type: 'openLightbox', slide: id, controls: boolean }`;
  - `{ type: 'closeLightbox' }`.
- **Custom tabs:** `lightbox: { slide: id, controls: boolean } | null`. Null runs triggers, as in P8.2.
- **Validation:** a lightbox slide is a scene slide, not a draw slide.
- **Deleting a slide** removes the triggers that open it (as it does for jumps to it) and sets a custom tab that opened it back to running triggers.
- **Migration 22 → 23:** `lightbox: null` on every custom tab.
- **Legacy fixture:** `v22-two-slides`.
- **A new label:** `lightbox` ("Lightbox"), the lightbox bar's name.

## Runtime

- **Engine:**
  - `openLightbox(slideId, controls)` and `closeLightbox()`, for actions and for the player;
  - `next`, `previous` and the view's `canNext` and `canPrevious` follow rule 5;
  - `view.lightbox` is `{ underSlideId, controls } | null`;
  - `view.slideId` is the slide being played (the lightbox's);
  - `view.slideIndex` and the saved session are the slide under it.
- **Player:**
  - the slide under the lightbox stays drawn, still and dimmed, behind a smaller framed stage that plays the lightbox slide;
  - a "Lightbox" bar gives Close and, with controls, Previous and Next;
  - Escape closes it;
  - its sound and video play, and the slide under it is silent.

## Editor

- **The trigger wizard:**
  - "Open a slide as a lightbox", with the slide and a "Show Previous and Next" checkbox;
  - "Close the lightbox".
- **The Player dialog's custom tabs:** a "When clicked" choice: run the slide's triggers, or open a slide as a lightbox (with the slide, and the controls checkbox).

## Done when

- **Model:**
  - the actions and tab lightboxes validate (scene slides only, no draw slides);
  - migration 22 → 23 and the fixture;
  - deleting a slide clears what opened it;
  - the property test opens and closes lightboxes.
- **Engine:**
  - opening freezes the slide under it (timeline, media, states) and plays the lightbox from 0 with its own layers;
  - variables are shared;
  - closing returns to the slide as it was, with no new `timeline starts`;
  - controls on and off for next and previous;
  - a jump from inside closes it first;
  - one lightbox at a time;
  - the session saves the slide under it.
- **Player browser (Chromium and Firefox):**
  - a button opens a lightbox over the frozen slide;
  - the Lightbox bar's Close and Escape close it;
  - Previous and Next with controls, and none without;
  - a custom tab opens one;
  - the slide count shows the slide under it.
- **e2e:**
  - in the editor, a button whose trigger opens slide 3 as a lightbox with controls, and a custom tab that opens slide 2;
  - save and reopen;
  - publish, and use both in the course.

## Not in this package

- Lightbox sizes or positions of their own: it is drawn at 80% of the stage, centred.
