# P6.7 Zoom regions and the cue-point workflow

**Work package:** P6.7, needs P6.1 (audio) and P3.3 (timeline), both done. Plan line: zoom region objects on the timeline; "align to cue point"; add cue points while audio plays (keyboard shortcut).

## Zoom regions

A zoom region is an object with a frame and a time on the timeline, like any other. In a course it draws nothing. While it is on stage, the learner's view of the slide zooms in to it.

### Saved format version 11

- A new object type, `zoomRegion`, with `props: { speed }`. `speed` is the seconds the zoom in and the zoom out take (0–3, default 0.5).
- It may sit on a slide's base layer or an overlay. It may not be inside a group, and it has no animations; validation reports either.
- It has no states other than `normal`, and takes no input.
- No migration is needed beyond the version bump (no existing object changes). There is a legacy fixture `v10-two-slides`, as for every bump.

### Runtime

- **`EngineView.camera`:** the part of the stage shown, `{ x, y, width, height }` in stage units. It is absent when the whole stage is shown. It is worked out from the playheads, so it pauses, seeks and scrubs with them.
- **For one region on a layer:**
  - the view zooms in over the first `speed` seconds of its bar;
  - it stays zoomed in;
  - it zooms back out over the last `speed` seconds, so it is back as its bar ends. This matches Luke's decision on exits (2026-11-18).
  - Both moves ease in and out.
- **Overlapping regions:** the later-starting one wins, and zooms from wherever the view was when it started. As it ends, the view goes back to the region still on stage, if there is one.
- **Layers:** the topmost shown layer with a region on stage decides the view.
- **How a region is fitted:** the region's middle is put in the middle of the view, scaled as far as the region fits whole (`min(W/w, H/h)`). A region of a different shape from the stage shows a little more than itself.
- This adds behaviour for the new object type only. Nothing in plan §5 changes.

### Drawing

- **The player** applies the camera to the slide's container with the CSS `scale` and `translate` properties. These sit outside the `transform` that slide transitions animate, so the two do not fight.
- **The editor stage** draws a zoom region as a dashed frame labelled "Zoom" (render's `mediaMarkers` option, which now means "editor markers"). It is not zoomed while scrubbing: the author sees the region itself. A course and thumbnails draw nothing for it.

### Editor

- Insert → **Zoom Region** adds one at the playhead for 3 s (to the timeline's end, if sooner), at half the stage's size, in the middle.
- The Format tab shows **Zoom speed** for a selected region.
- It is resized and moved like any object, and its bar is dragged on the timeline like any other.

## Cue-point workflow

- **Playing sound in the editor.** Play on the timeline now plays, from the playhead:
  - the layer's sounds that start with the timeline;
  - the sound of its videos that start with the timeline.
  - Each sound plays while the playhead is inside its bar, at its own volume, with a video's trim start allowed for. A sound more than 0.15 s out of step is put back in step.
  - Pause stops them.
- **C adds a cue point** at the playhead while the timeline plays, named "Cue N".
  - It does nothing when typing in a field.
  - The **+ Cue** button still adds one at the playhead at any time.
- **Align to cue point:** a list in the timeline toolbar of the layer's cue points. Choosing one starts the selected objects at that cue's time, as one undo step, keeping their durations.

## Done when

- **Model:** zoom region schema and validation (not in a group, no animations), the legacy fixture, and the property test inserting regions.
- **Engine:**
  - camera at times: in, held and out;
  - overlap and handing back;
  - a layer's region over the base's;
  - none when no region is on stage;
  - paused and seeked.
- **Render:** a region draws nothing in a course and a marker on the stage.
- **Player browser test (Chromium and Firefox):** the slide is scaled and moved to the region part way and when in, and back afterwards.
- **e2e:**
  - insert a zoom region, set its speed, save and reopen;
  - publish and see the view zoomed in the course;
  - insert a sound, press Play and see the sound playing in the editor, press C twice to add two cue points while it plays, and check them;
  - align an object to a cue point, with undo.

## Not in this package

- Zooming the editor stage while scrubbing.
- Zoom regions on masters.
- A zoom that follows a moving object.
- Snapping objects to cue points while dragging (the timeline has its own snapping later).
