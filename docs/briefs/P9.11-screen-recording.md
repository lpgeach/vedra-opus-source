# P9.11 Screen recording

**Work package:** P9.11. It needs P9.10 (PR #79).

**Plan line:** screen recording inserted as video. Step-by-step "try" and "test" modes are a later stretch, and are not in this package.

**My design choices** (Luke asked to go through all of Phase 9), listed for him to change:

1. **Insert → Screen Recording…** opens a dialog.
   - It lists what can be recorded: each screen and each window, with a small picture of it.
   - The author picks one, and may also record narration from the microphone.
   - Record starts after a three-second count, and Stop ends it.
   - The author can watch the take back, record again, or insert it.
2. **Inserting** puts the take through the media pipeline (P6.3), as an imported video is: WebM (VP9, with Opus when there is narration) with a poster frame. It goes on the slide as a video object, fitted like any video.
3. **Where the recording happens:** all on the machine. The take is held in memory until it is inserted, then stored as a project asset.
   - Nothing is uploaded.
   - The pictures of screens and windows are shown in the dialog only, and never saved.
4. **Permissions:** the editor may capture a screen or window only through this dialog.
   - The main process remembers the source the author picked, and gives it to the next capture the editor asks for.
   - Any other capture request is refused, and so is any request from Preview windows.
   - The microphone rule (P6.1) is unchanged.
   - On macOS, the system asks for screen-recording permission the first time.
5. **The take's limits:** at most 10 minutes, and 500 MB.
   - At 10 minutes it stops by itself and says so.
   - The editor window is not hidden while recording. The author chooses another window or screen, or records what they like.
6. **Not in this package:**
   - recording the system's own sound;
   - a global shortcut to stop;
   - cursor highlighting;
   - "try" and "test" simulation modes;
   - editing the take beyond the video trimming P6.2 already gives.

## Saved format

No change: a recording is a video asset and a video object, as P6.2 already has.

## IPC (main ↔ renderer)

- `screen:sources` gives the screens and windows, each with an id, a name, a kind and a small picture as a data URL.
- `screen:choose(id | null)` sets the source the next capture request gets, or clears it.
- `project:storeScreenRecording(bytes)` takes the WebM through the pipeline and returns the video asset and its poster.

## Done when

- **Main:**
  - the capture handler gives the chosen source once, then refuses;
  - it refuses with nothing chosen, and refuses Preview windows;
  - the size limit holds;
  - `storeScreenRecording` makes a video asset with a poster through the pipeline (with ffmpeg present).
- **e2e:**
  - Insert → Screen Recording… lists sources (stubbed in the test, so nothing real is captured or shown);
  - pick one, record a few seconds (the capture stubbed with a moving canvas), stop, watch back, insert;
  - a video object is on the slide, with its asset in the Media panel as WebM;
  - publish, and it plays.
