# P6.6 Slide and layer transitions

**Work package:** P6.6, needs P6.5 (done). Plan line: fade, push, cover, uncover, and similar, with duration and direction.

## What a transition is

- **A slide transition** plays when the learner arrives at that slide from another one (Next, Previous, a jump). The old slide is shown going away and the new one arriving.
  - None plays on the course's first slide, or when a session is resumed.
- **A layer transition** plays when an overlay layer is shown, and in reverse when it is hidden. A hidden layer stays drawn until its transition has played out, but takes no input.
- **Transitions only draw.** They change nothing in plan §5:
  - the slide's timeline starts on entry as before;
  - triggers run at once;
  - the old slide has already been left, so it takes no input and its media has stopped.

## Saved format version 10

`slide.properties.transition` and `layer.properties.transition` (on overlays; the base layer's is always null):

```ts
Transition {
  kind: 'fade' | 'push' | 'cover' | 'uncover' | 'wipe' | 'split' | 'iris' | 'zoom',
  duration: number,          // seconds, 0.1–5
  direction: Direction | null,
}
```

- **What each kind does.** `direction` is where the new slide or layer comes from (left, right, top, bottom), or how a split opens (horizontal, vertical).

  | Kind      | What happens                                        | Directions               |
  | --------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
  | `fade`    | the new one fades in over the old                   | none                     |
  | `push`    | the new one slides in and pushes the old one out    | right, left, bottom, top |
  | `cover`   | the new one slides in over the old, which stays     | right, left, bottom, top |
  | `uncover` | the old one slides away, revealing the new beneath  | right, left, bottom, top |
  | `wipe`    | the new one is revealed from one side               | left, right, top, bottom |
  | `split`   | the new one is revealed from the middle outwards    | horizontal, vertical     |
  | `iris`    | the new one is revealed by a circle from the centre | none                     |
  | `zoom`    | the new one grows from the centre while fading in   | none                     |

  The first direction listed is the default. For `uncover`, the direction says where the _old_ slide goes (towards it).

- **Validation:** the direction must be one the kind takes, and a base layer's transition must be null.
- **Migration 9 → 10** sets `transition: null` on every slide's properties and every layer's (slides, overlays, masters, layouts). Legacy fixture `v9-two-slides`.
- **Editing** goes through `slide.setProperties` and `layer.setProperties`.

## Runtime (engine)

- **`EngineView.transition`**: `{ kind, direction, duration, time, fromSlideId }` while a slide transition is under way. `time` is seconds in; it is absent otherwise.
- **`LayerView.transition`**: `{ phase: 'in' | 'out', kind, direction, duration, time }` while an overlay's is under way.
  - A layer being hidden stays in `view.layers`, with `shown: false`, until its out transition ends.
  - It takes no input, its media is stopped, and its timeline does not move.
- **Time:** transitions run on the engine clock (`tick`), whatever the timelines are doing. Pausing a timeline does not pause one.
  - Showing a layer again during its out transition starts the in transition.
  - Leaving the slide ends every transition at once.
- **Pure:** the engine only reports times; drawing is the player's job.

## Drawing

- **`render/src/transition.ts`:** `transitionKeyframes(transition, stage)` gives `{ incoming, outgoing, easing }`.
  - Keyframes are for the new and the old slide (or layer) containers. `outgoing` is null when the old one does not move.
  - Pure and unit-tested. A hide plays the same keyframes backwards on the layer, with nothing outgoing.
- **Player:**
  - on a slide change with a transition, it keeps the old slide's drawing (moved, not redrawn) in a container beneath the new one;
  - it animates both with paused Web Animations whose `currentTime` it sets from `view.transition.time`;
  - it removes the old drawing when the transition ends;
  - layer containers animate the same way;
  - the stage clips to its box, so pushed slides do not show outside it.
- **Editor:** the Transitions ribbon tab has a _Preview_ button. It plays the transition on the stage once, from a blank stage (or, for a layer, from the slide beneath it), using the same keyframes.

## Editor UI: the Transitions tab

- **Slide transition** (the slide being edited): kind (None or one of the above), direction, and duration. _Apply to all slides_ gives every slide in the project the same transition, as one undo step.
- **Layer transition:** while an overlay layer is being edited, a second group sets that layer's transition.
- **Slide list:** a slide with a transition shows a small mark on its thumbnail (`data-transition`).
- **Master View:** the tab says transitions belong to slides.

## Done when

- **Model:** schema, directions per kind, a base layer's transition must be null, migration 9 → 10 with the legacy fixture, and `slide.setProperties` and `layer.setProperties` with transitions in the property test.
- **Engine:**
  - a slide transition's view and time on next, previous and jump, and its end;
  - none on the first slide or on resume;
  - it runs while the timeline is paused;
  - a layer's in and out transitions;
  - a layer being hidden stays in the view, not shown, and refuses input until its out transition ends;
  - showing it again during the out transition starts the in transition;
  - navigating away clears transitions.
- **Render:** keyframes for every kind and direction (the numbers).
- **Player browser tests (Chromium and Firefox):**
  - a fade's opacity part way;
  - a push with the old slide still drawn and moving, and gone afterwards;
  - a layer fading in and out;
  - clicks not reaching a layer being hidden.
- **e2e:**
  - set a slide transition and _Apply to all_;
  - set a layer's transition;
  - see the thumbnail mark;
  - Preview plays on the stage;
  - save and reopen;
  - publish and see both slides drawn part way through a push.

## Not in this package

- Transitions on lightbox slides (P8).
- Sounds with transitions.
- Easing choices.
- Transitions when jumping to a slide in another scene that differ by scene.
- More kinds (blinds, dissolve and the like).
