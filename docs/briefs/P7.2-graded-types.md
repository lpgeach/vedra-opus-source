# P7.2 Graded question types

**Work package:** P7.2, needs P7.1 (done).

**Plan line:** true/false, multiple choice, multiple response, fill in the blank, word bank, matching (drag and drop, dropdown), sequence (drag and drop, dropdown), numeric, hotspot.

**How it builds on P7.1.** Multiple choice and multiple response came with P7.1. Each new type follows Luke's P7.1 decisions:

- its parts are ordinary objects on the slide;
- the form edits their words and the question's settings;
- the learner answers in the course and Submit marks it.

**Delivery.** This package is built in three steps on one branch, each committed when it passes:

- **A.** True/false, fill in the blank, numeric, matching (dropdown) and sequence (dropdown), with two new input objects: a text entry and a dropdown.
- **B.** Drag and drop at its simplest (an item dropped on a target snaps there, anywhere else it goes back), then word bank, matching (drag and drop) and sequence (drag and drop). P7.4's freeform drag and drop extends this rather than starting again.
- **C.** Hotspot.

## Saved format version 14

**`Question` becomes a union on `kind`.** Every kind shares:

- `prompt`, `submit`, `attempts`, `branch` and `required`;
- `scoring`: `{ by: 'question', points }`, plus `{ by: 'choice' }` for the choice kinds only;
- `feedback`, where `by: 'choice'` is allowed for the choice kinds only.

**Each kind's own parts.** "Object" means an ordinary object on the slide's base layer.

| Kind               | Its parts                                                                                 | Correct when                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `multipleChoice`   | `choices` (as P7.1), `shuffle`                                                            | the one correct choice is chosen                                                       |
| `multipleResponse` | `choices`, `shuffle`                                                                      | exactly the correct choices are chosen                                                 |
| `trueFalse`        | `choices`: exactly two, True and False, one correct                                       | the correct one is chosen                                                              |
| `fillInBlank`      | `entry` (a text entry object), `answers: string[]`, `caseSensitive`                       | the text typed, trimmed (and lower-cased unless case-sensitive), is one of the answers |
| `numeric`          | `entry`, `answers: Condition[]`                                                           | the number typed meets any one condition                                               |
| `matchingDropdown` | `pairs: { id, item, select, match }[]`, `shuffle`                                         | each pair's dropdown shows its own `match`                                             |
| `sequenceDropdown` | `steps: { id, select, text }[]`                                                           | the dropdown in position _i_ shows step _i_'s text                                     |
| `wordBank` (B)     | `words: { id, object, correct }[]`, `blank` (a drop target object)                        | a correct word is in the blank                                                         |
| `matchingDrag` (B) | `pairs: { id, item, target }[]`, `shuffle`                                                | each item is on its own target                                                         |
| `sequenceDrag` (B) | `steps: { id, object }[]`                                                                 | the objects stand in the steps' order                                                  |
| `hotspot` (C)      | `picture` (a shape the author can give an image fill), `spots: { id, object, correct }[]` | the learner's mark is inside a correct spot                                            |

The hotspot picture is a shape rather than an `image` object: a new question slide can then be made without importing an asset, and the author can give the shape an image fill in Slide View. Spots are shapes, shown to the author but not to the learner.

- **`Condition`** is `{ op: 'equal' | 'notEqual' | 'less' | 'lessOrEqual' | 'greater' | 'greaterOrEqual', value }`, or `{ op: 'between', from, to }` (both ends included).
- **True/false** is multiple choice with exactly two choices that cannot be added to or taken away.

**New object types:**

| Type        | Props                    | Editor and thumbnails         | Course                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `textEntry` | `placeholder`, `numeric` | a box showing the placeholder | an `<input>` (`inputmode=decimal` when numeric) |
| `dropdown`  | `options: string[]`      | a box showing "Choose…"       | a `<select>` of the options                     |

- They are drawn live only in a course, through the render context's `liveMedia`, which now means "live in a course".
- **Steps B and C** add `dragItem` and `dropTarget` (or a flag on ordinary objects; settled in step B's section) and the hotspot `spot`.

**Migration 13 → 14:** existing questions are unchanged; only the version moves. Legacy fixture `v13-two-slides`.

## Runtime

- **Learner input:**
  - `engine.input(objectId, value)` takes what the learner typed or chose in a text entry or dropdown;
  - the view reports it (`ObjectView.value`), and `ObjectView.locked` once the question is finished;
  - the player keeps the elements in step, and disables them when locked.
- **Marking:** `markAnswer` gains a case for each kind, by the table above.
  - **Unanswered** (nothing typed, or a dropdown left on "Choose…") raises `questionUnanswered`, as in P7.1.
  - **Partial answers** to matching and sequence count as wrong.
  - **Score** is by question for these kinds.
- **Shuffle:**
  - matching (dropdown) lists each dropdown's options in a shuffled order;
  - sequence (dropdown) always lists its options shuffled;
  - the order is kept in the session.
- **Session state:** each question's record gains `v`, its inputs by part position, and the suspend codec carries them (rule 17).

## Editor

- **Insert:** a command for each kind, as P7.1's: True/False, Fill in the Blank, Numeric, Matching (Dropdown), Sequence (Dropdown), and in later steps Word Bank, Matching (Drag), Sequence (Drag) and Hotspot.
- **Form View**, by kind:
  - **choice kinds:** as P7.1, with true/false's choices fixed;
  - **fill in the blank:** the acceptable answers (add and delete) and case-sensitive;
  - **numeric:** the conditions (operator and value or values; add and delete);
  - **matching:** pairs of an item's words and its match's words (add, delete, move);
  - **sequence:** the steps in their right order (add, delete, move).
- **The type select** offers only the kinds that share parts: multiple choice and multiple response, as in P7.1. Other kinds are chosen by inserting them.

## Done when (step A; B and C add theirs)

- **Model:** the union validates every kind's parts and their object types; migration 13 → 14 and the fixture; the property test inserting each kind.
- **Engine:**
  - marking for true/false, fill in the blank (case and trimming), numeric (every operator, between inclusive), matching dropdown and sequence dropdown (all, part, none);
  - unanswered submits;
  - input refused once finished;
  - inputs kept on revisit and resume;
  - the suspend codec round trip.
- **Render:** a text entry and a dropdown drawn in the editor and live.
- **Player browser (Chromium and Firefox):** fill in the blank typed and marked; numeric typed and marked; matching dropdown chosen and marked; locked after finishing.
- **e2e:** insert a fill-in-the-blank and a matching (dropdown) question; set them up in Form View; save and reopen; publish and answer both in the course.

## Done when (step B)

- **Model:** `wordBank`, `matchingDrag` and `sequenceDrag` validate their parts and object types; inserting, deleting and copying them in the property test keeps ids unique and references sound.
- **Engine:** `drop` snaps a word into the blank, items onto their targets and swaps sequence tiles on their places; a drop elsewhere returns the tile; `markAnswer` gives full marks only for a correct blank, every item on its own target, and the steps in order; partial answers are wrong; tiles are locked after finishing; placements survive revisit, resume and the suspend codec.
- **Player browser:** a word drops in the blank, items onto targets, steps into order; a press without movement is still a click; a carried tile follows the pointer; finished tiles no longer move.
- **Editor:** the three kinds are in Insert → Question; Form View edits words, matches and the right order; the slide shows their shapes.
- **e2e:** author all three, save and reopen, publish, then answer all three in the course.

## Done when (step C)

- **Model:** `hotspot` validates its picture and spots; inserting, deleting spots and copying it in the property test keeps ids unique and references sound.
- **Engine:** `mark(x, y)` refuses a click outside the picture or after finishing; it resolves the spot under the mark; Submit marks a mark in a correct spot right, any other spot or a mark outside them wrong; a wrong mark can be retried; spots are hidden from the learner; the mark survives revisit, resume and the suspend codec.
- **Player:** a click on the picture places a mark at that point in stage units, drawn over the slide; the mark is locked with the question.
- **Editor:** Hotspot is in Insert → Question; Form View adds and deletes spots and ticks the correct ones; the slide shows the picture and the author's guides.
- **Player browser and e2e:** a mark on a wrong spot can be retried, a mark on a right one moves on, and the mark is kept on return; the e2e authors a hotspot, saves and reopens it, publishes and answers it in the course.
