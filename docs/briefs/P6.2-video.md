# P6.2 Video

**Work package:** P6.2, needs P6.1 (done). Plan line: import mp4/webm; poster; trim in/out; show controls or not; play on timeline start or on click; picture-in-picture off by default.

Video reuses P6.1's media model (`docs/briefs/P6.1-audio.md`): the same engine media states, the same actions and event, and the same time-master rule. The plan's clock rule already names video ("audio or video that spans the timeline"). This brief covers what is different: a video is seen, has a picture to stand in for it, can be trimmed, can have controls, and can be started by clicking it.

## Saved format version 7

```ts
{
  ...objectBase,
  type: 'video',
  props: {
    asset: AssetHash,           // kind 'video'
    length: number,             // seconds of the whole file, measured on insert
    trimStart: number,          // seconds into the file where playing starts
    trimEnd: number,            // and ends; trimStart < trimEnd <= length
    volume: number,             // 0–1
    startOn: 'timeline' | 'click' | 'trigger',
    controls: boolean,          // the browser's own controls in the course
    pictureInPicture: boolean,  // default false
    poster: AssetHash | null,   // an image shown before playing; null: the first frame
  },
}
```

- **Played length** is `trimEnd − trimStart`. All engine times are within that range: 0 is `trimStart`.
- **Media actions and `mediaCompletes`** accept audio or video. Validation:
  - the asset must be a video, and the poster an image;
  - `0 ≤ trimStart < trimEnd ≤ length`.
- **Migration 6 → 7** adds nothing. Legacy fixture `fixtures/legacy/v6-two-slides`.

## Runtime (engine)

- **Video objects are media,** with the P6.1 states and rules: `follow` with the timeline, `free`, `paused`, `stopped`, and resets. The time master can be audio or video, whichever playing `follow` object starts earliest.
- **`startOn: 'click'`** starts `stopped`, like `trigger`. A learner's click on the video plays it if it is not playing (`free`, from where it is) and pauses it if it is. That is built-in behaviour, like states. The click's triggers still run afterwards. If the video shows its controls, a click is left to them, because the browser itself plays and pauses a video with controls when it is clicked; toggling in the engine too would undo the click. Found while building it.
- **A stall limit for time masters** (found in P6.2, applies to audio too): a master that reports without moving on holds its timeline for at most 2 s (`MAX_STALL`). After that the clock moves the timeline until the media moves again. Without it, media that cannot play on would freeze the course for ever. Firefox, for one, sticks after a seek in a WebM without cues.
- **The learner's own controls:** when the browser's controls start, pause or move the video, the player tells the engine with `engine.mediaControl(objectId, playing, seconds)`. The video becomes `free` (playing) or `paused` at that place, and no longer follows the timeline. What the learner does wins.

## Drawing

- **`render` draws a video object** as a `<video>` filling its frame (`object-fit: contain`, black behind). It has:
  - its poster image when it has one;
  - `preload="metadata"`, and the source address ending `#t=<trimStart>`, so without a poster the frame at the trim start shows;
  - `muted`, `playsinline`, and `disablepictureinpicture` unless `pictureInPicture`;
  - `data-media-id`.
- **In the editor** (the default) it shows no controls and takes no pointer input: it is a picture of the video.
- **Players** create their context with `liveMedia: true`. The video then gets `controls` when the object asks for them, and the player plays this very element.
- **Redraws keep the element:** when a video object's state changes, the player updates the object's box but keeps its `<video>`, so playback is not interrupted.

## Player

The P6.1 soundboard becomes the media board:

- **Audio:** detached audio elements, as before.
- **Video:** the `<video>` elements on the stage, found by `data-media-id`. Element time is `trimStart` plus engine time, and the board stops a video that passes `trimEnd`.
- **The learner's controls:** `play`, `pause` and `seeked` events the board did not cause itself go to `engine.mediaControl`.
- **Sound:** the board unmutes a video when it plays.
- **Blocked autoplay** works as for audio: the course waits behind Play.

## Editor

- **Insert → Video…** (mp4, webm), and Media panel → Insert for video assets.
  - **Measuring:** the editor loads the video's metadata for its length and picture size. A file with no recorded duration (some WebM) is measured by seeking to its end.
  - **Placing:** it goes on the slide centred, scaled down to fit like a picture, on the timeline from 0 s for its whole length.
- **The timeline grows to fit media:** inserting audio or video longer than what is left of the layer's timeline lengthens the timeline to fit, in the same undo step. This also fixes a gap in P6.1: a 60 s narration on a 5 s slide stopped at 5 s.
- **Format panel → Video:**
  - Volume.
  - Starts: "With the timeline", "When clicked", "When a trigger plays it".
  - Show controls.
  - Allow picture-in-picture.
  - Trim: start and end, in seconds. Changing the trim sets the object's duration to the new played length.
  - Poster: "First frame" or any image in the project.
  - Length.
- **The trigger wizard** lists videos among the media for Play, Pause and Stop, and "media completes".

## Done when

- **Engine unit tests:** video as media (follow, trimmed length, completion), click to play and pause, `mediaControl` from controls, master selection across audio and video.
- **Model:** the object validates; bad trim, wrong asset kind and wrong poster kind are refused; migration 6 → 7 through the frozen fixture; the property test adds videos.
- **Render:**
  - unit tests for attributes: poster, fragment, controls only live, picture-in-picture;
  - a visual test of a video with a poster.
- **Player browser tests** (Chromium and Firefox), with a WebM the test makes itself (canvas plus tone, recorded in Chromium):
  - a trimmed video plays from `trimStart` and completes at `trimEnd`;
  - click to play and pause;
  - controls reach the engine;
  - the timeline follows the video;
  - a state change does not restart it.
- **e2e:**
  - insert the WebM: its size, length, and the timeline lengthened to fit;
  - set trim, start on click, controls, and a poster;
  - save and reopen;
  - publish, then play by clicking the video;
  - audio longer than the timeline lengthens it.
