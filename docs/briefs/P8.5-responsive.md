# P8.5 Responsive behaviour

**Work package:** P8.5. It needs P8.1 to P8.4.

**Plan line:** scale to fit any viewport; touch input and gestures; mobile chrome that collapses the sidebar; orientation handling.

**What is there already:**

- the stage scales to fit the space it has, keeping its shape (since P3);
- taps reach objects as clicks;
- drag questions take touch (P7.2);
- the published page sets `width=device-width`.

P8.5 makes the frame itself work on a phone or a small window.

**My design choices** (Luke asked to go through all of Phase 8), listed for him to change:

1. **Compact frame:** below 640 CSS pixels wide, the player is compact (`data-compact`).
   - The course title shortens with an ellipsis, and the tab buttons scroll sideways rather than wrap.
   - The menu and the tab panels take the full width: the sidebar collapses into an overlay that covers the course.
   - Previous, the slide count and Next stay in the footer. The seekbar, volume, speed, accessibility and full screen move behind a **More** button (`aria-expanded`), which opens them in a row above the footer.
2. **Touch targets:** on a coarse pointer (`pointer: coarse`), every frame button is at least 44 × 44 pixels.
3. **Swipe:** a quick sideways swipe with a finger on the course goes to the next slide (swiping left) or the previous one (swiping right).
   - It follows the same rules as the arrow keys: a button that is hidden or disabled does nothing, and in a lightbox it moves the lightbox only when the lightbox has Previous and Next.
   - It is ignored when the swipe starts on something that takes the pointer: a draggable tile, a text field or a control.
   - Only touch swipes count, never the mouse.
   - The rule: at least 60 pixels sideways, more than twice as far sideways as up or down, within 600 ms.
4. **Orientation:** when the course is landscape, the screen is portrait and the player is compact, a hint says "Turn your device sideways for a bigger view."
   - It has a Dismiss button, and it does not come back for that page.
   - It never blocks the course.
   - The root also carries `data-orientation`, for styling.
5. **No new project settings.** All of this is how the player behaves. Only the new words are saved, as labels:
   - `more` ("More");
   - `rotateHint` (the hint);
   - `dismiss` ("Dismiss").

## Saved format version 24

- The label keys `more`, `rotateHint` and `dismiss`.
- **Migration 23 → 24:** nothing to change. A version 23 file is valid as it is; the bump stops an older Vedra Opus from rejecting newer words.
- **Legacy fixture:** `v23-two-slides`.

## Runtime (player only)

- **`packages/player/src/responsive.ts`:**
  - `isCompact(width)`;
  - `swipeOf(dx, dy, ms)` (`'next' | 'previous' | null`);
  - `wantsRotation(stage, viewport, compact)`;
  - `attachResponsive(root, …)`: a ResizeObserver on the root sets `data-compact` and `data-orientation`, shows or hides the hint, and turns touch swipes into Next and Previous.
- The viewport has `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom`, so a sideways swipe reaches the player and the browser keeps its own pinch zoom.

## Done when

- **Player (unit):** `isCompact`, `swipeOf` (distance, angle, time, mouse ignored), `wantsRotation`.
- **Player browser (Chromium and Firefox):**
  - the stage fits, keeping its shape, at phone and desktop sizes;
  - compact below 640 pixels: More shows and hides the extra controls, and the menu covers the full width;
  - a touch swipe changes slides, a mouse drag does not, and a swipe on a drag tile does not;
  - a swipe respects hidden Next;
  - the rotation hint shows for a landscape course in a portrait compact player, and Dismiss hides it for good.
- **e2e:** publish a course from the app and open it in a phone-sized touch browser: compact chrome, More, a swipe to the next slide, and the rotation hint.

## Not in this package

- Layouts made for phones by the author (other stage sizes per device).
- Pinch zoom of our own.
