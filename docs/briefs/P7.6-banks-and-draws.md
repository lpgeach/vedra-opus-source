# P7.6 Question banks and draws

**Work package:** P7.6, needs P7.5 (done, merged in PR #58).

**Plan line:** banks; draw N questions randomly or in order; lock specific questions into a draw.

**What it is:**

- A **question bank** holds question slides outside the course's scenes. A bank's slides are never played where they are.
- A **draw slide** sits in a scene where the questions should come. When the session starts it is replaced by questions drawn from a bank:
  - N of them;
  - chosen at random or in the bank's order;
  - always including the questions the author locked into the draw.

These are my design choices, made without asking (Luke said to continue), and listed for Luke to change:

1. **Banks are shaped like scenes:** a title and slides, kept in `project.banks`. Only question slides may be in a bank.
2. **The draw is made once per session,** for every draw slide, when the course starts. It is saved in the session, so a resumed course has the same questions.
3. **A bank slide is drawn at most once per session.** A second draw from the same bank takes from what is left.
4. **Locked questions are always drawn.** If more are locked than N, all the locked ones are drawn.
5. **Order:**
   - "in order" keeps the bank's order;
   - "random" picks at random and then shuffles everything drawn, the locked ones included.
6. **Jumping to a draw slide** goes to its first drawn question. A draw that drew nothing is passed over.

## Saved format version 18

**Banks:** `project.banks: { id, title, slides: Slide[] }[]`.

- Validation checks that every bank slide is a question slide with no draw.
- Ids are unique project-wide, as everywhere.

**Draws:** `slide.draw: Draw | null` (null on every other slide):

```ts
Draw = {
  bank: id | null,                 // null: draws nothing (its bank was deleted)
  count: int 1…100,
  order: 'random' | 'inOrder',
  locked: slideId[],               // slides of that bank
}
```

- A draw slide is in a scene, has no question, and its own objects are not played.
- Jump targets can name scene slides only, a draw slide among them, but not bank slides.

**Traversal:**

- `allSlides` stays the course's scene slides, in order.
- `everySlide` adds the bank slides after them. Validation, references, layers and finding by id use it.

**Commands:**

- `bank.add`, `bank.remove` and `bank.rename`;
- `slide.add` and `slide.move` take a bank's id as their `sceneId`, for question slides only;
- `slide.setDraw`;
- removing a bank sets the draws from it to `bank: null`;
- removing a bank slide takes it out of every draw's `locked`.

**Migration 17 → 18:** `banks: []`, and `draw: null` on every slide. Legacy fixture `v17-two-slides`.

## Runtime

**The course order:** the scene slides, each draw slide replaced by its drawn slides. Navigation, Next and Previous, slide numbers, the visited flags and the question records all work on that order.

**The draw itself** (`drawQuestions(draw, bank, taken, random)`, pure):

1. take the locked slides first, then fill up to `count` from the rest of the bank, in bank order or at random;
2. leave out slides already drawn this session;
3. shuffle the whole draw when the order is random.

**Session state:** `SessionState.d: Record<drawSlideId, slideId[]>`.

- A restored session uses its saved draws, dropping ids no longer in the bank. A draw that has lost all its slides is drawn again.
- The suspend codec packs each draw as slide positions in `everySlide` order (rule 17).

**Tracking** counts the slides in the course order, so a draw of 3 counts 3 slides.

## Editor

**The slide list** (as built; Overview does not show banks yet):

- a "Question Banks" section after the scenes, listing each bank and its slides;
- New Question Bank, a name field to rename, and Delete;
- a bank slide is edited like any other: Form View and Slide View;
- draw slides carry a mark (⇉).

**Making questions:**

- a new bank starts with one multiple-choice question;
- Insert → Question, with a bank slide current, adds the new question to that bank.

**Insert → Question → "Draw from Question Bank":**

- adds a draw slide after the current slide;
- if there is no bank yet, it makes one, with a question in it.

**Form View for a draw slide** (the Draw form):

- the bank;
- how many to draw;
- the order;
- a "Locked" checkbox for each of the bank's questions, by title.

On the stage, a draw slide shows a note saying what it draws.

  - a bank slide removed since the save.

  - make a bank of three questions;

  - save and reopen;
  - publish, and play through the two drawn questions, the locked one among them.
