# P8.3 Player appearance and text labels

**Work package:** P8.3. It needs P8.1 (the frame) and P8.2 (the tabs).

**Plan line:** colours, fonts, light/dark, layout of controls, editable label strings per language (foundation for P10 localisation), saved player presets.

**What it is.** The frame from P8.1 and P8.2 gets settings for how it looks:

- **colours:** a light or dark scheme, or the learner's system setting, with an accent colour of the author's;
- **a font;**
- **where the controls and the menu go;**
- **every word it shows:** editable, per language, so P10 can add translations.

**Presets** save a look to reuse in other courses.

**My design choices** (Luke asked to go through all of Phase 8), listed for him to change:

1. **The look is a few settings, not a stylesheet:**
   - **scheme:** dark, light, or auto (the learner's system setting);
   - **accent:** one colour, for the current menu entry, focus rings, an open tab and pressed buttons. Its text is black or white, whichever reads better on it.
   - **font:** the system's, or the course theme's body or heading font;
   - **controls:** under the course or above it;
   - **menu:** at the left or the right. The tab panel takes the other side.

   A new course looks as courses do today: dark, blue accent, system font, controls under, menu left.

2. **The words.** Every word the frame shows has a key: Previous, Next, "Slide {n} of {total}", the menu, the tabs, the accessibility panel, the resume question, the review lines, the timer, and the rest. A course's labels are:
   - its **language** (a BCP 47 tag, `en` for a new course), which the player also puts on the page as `lang`;
   - **for each language, only the words the author changed.** Anything not changed shows the built-in English.

   Choosing another language keeps the words already written for each language. Vedra Opus ships no translations: a wrong one is worse than English, and translation is P10.

   Placeholders like `{n}` stay as written. A label that leaves one out simply does not show that value.

3. **Course content is not a label.** Slide text, question words and the Feedback master's words are the author's own and are edited where they are.
4. **Presets belong to the installation, not the project.** They live in the app's settings, beside the editor layout, so every project on the machine can use them.
   - A preset holds the look, the labels, the controls, the course title and logo switches, and the menu's show and navigation settings.
   - It never holds course content: no menu entries, no resources or glossary, no custom tabs, and no logo picture.
   - Applying a preset is one undo step.

## Saved format version 22

```ts
player.look: {
  scheme: 'dark' | 'light' | 'auto',
  accent: '#rrggbb',
  font: 'system' | 'body' | 'heading',
  controlsAt: 'bottom' | 'top',
  menuSide: 'left' | 'right',
}
player.labels: {
  language: string,                                     // BCP 47
  words: Record<language, Partial<Record<LabelKey, string>>>,
}
```

- **Migration 21 → 22:** the defaults above, with `words: {}`.
- **Legacy fixture:** `v21-two-slides`.
- **The model exports:**
  - `LABEL_KEYS` and `DEFAULT_LABELS` (the English);
  - `labelText(player, key, values)`: the author's word for the course language, or the English, with `{placeholders}` filled.
- **Presets:** `playerPresetSchema`, with `presetOf(player)` and `withPreset(player, preset)`.

## Runtime

- **The player:**
  - sets `data-scheme`, the accent and its text colour as CSS variables, the font, `data-controls` and `data-menu-side` on its root, and `lang`;
  - takes every word it shows from `labelText`.
- **The stylesheet** uses variables for its colours, a dark and a light set; `auto` follows `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Editor

The Player dialog gets two more sections:

- **Look:**
  - scheme, accent colour, font, where the controls and the menu go;
  - presets: choose one and Apply it, Save the current look as a preset with a name, or Delete one.
- **Words:**
  - the course language;
  - every label, its English shown as the placeholder; blank uses the English.

Every change is one undo step. Presets are kept in `settings.json` (no new IPC: the settings calls carry them).

## Done when

- **Model:**
  - look and labels validate (colour, language tag, label keys);
  - migration 21 → 22 and the fixture;
  - `labelText` falls back to English and fills placeholders;
  - `presetOf` and `withPreset` round-trip and leave course content alone;
  - the property test sets the look, words and presets.
- **Settings:** presets survive a load, and a damaged preset list falls back to none.
- **Player (unit):** the accent's text colour, and the labels used.
- **Player browser (Chromium and Firefox):**
  - light and dark change the frame's colours, and `auto` follows the browser's scheme;
  - the accent shows on the current menu entry;
  - controls at the top come before the stage;
  - the menu at the right;
  - the font;
  - changed words and the course language show (`lang`).
- **e2e:**
  - in the Player dialog, choose light, an accent, controls at the top, and change Next's word;
  - save it as a preset;
  - in a new project, apply the preset;
  - publish, and see it in the course.

## Not in this package

- Translations and per-learner language choice (P10).
- Responsive layout (P8.5).
- Free-form player stylesheets.
