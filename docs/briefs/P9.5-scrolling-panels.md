# P9.5 Scrolling panels

**Work package:** P9.5. It needs P9.4 (PR #73).

**Plan line:** scrolling panels.

**What it is:** a part of the slide that shows only a window of what it holds, and scrolls the rest into view, such as a long passage of text or a list of items beside a picture.

**My design choices** (Luke asked to go through all of Phase 9), listed for him to change:

1. **A scrolling panel is a group that scrolls.** Groups already hold objects, move and transform them together, and keep their triggers and states. A group gets one more setting: `scroll: { height } | null`.
   - With it, the panel shows the group's top `height` stage pixels, its full width, and scrolls through the rest.
   - Without it, the group is as before.
2. **Only up and down.** The panel is as wide as its group, and scrolls vertically.
3. **The window's height is the author's.** If the group is later made shorter than its window, the window is the group's height. Nothing turns invalid.
4. **In the editor**, every object in the panel is drawn, so the parts below the window can still be selected and edited. The window shows as a dashed outline labelled "Scroll window".
   - **Insert → Scrolling Panel** groups the selected objects and turns scrolling on, with a window half the group's height (at least 40).
   - The Format panel of any group turns scrolling on or off and sets the window's height.
5. **In a course:**
   - the panel clips to its window and scrolls by the wheel, a drag of the scroll bar, touch, or the keyboard;
   - it is a region in the tab order, named by its alt text or its name;
   - while it has focus, Up, Down, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End scroll it instead of changing slides;
   - what is inside works as it does anywhere: buttons, fields, triggers;
   - thumbnails clip to the window too, without scrolling.
