# P6.4 Closed captions

**Work package:** P6.4, needs P6.1 and P6.2 (done). Plan line: import and export VTT/SRT; caption editor synced to the waveform; captions toggle in the player.

## Saved format version 8

Audio and video objects get `props.captions`: a list of cues.

```ts
CaptionCue {
  id: Id,
  start: number,   // seconds into the media file
  end: number,     // > start, within the file
  text: string,    // may hold line breaks
}
```

- **Times are in the file's own time,** as VTT and SRT files are, so an imported file lines up whatever the trim. For a trimmed video, the cue at file time _t_ shows when the video is at `trimStart + (time played)` = _t_.
- **Validation:** `start < end`, and `end ≤ length` (the file's measured length).
- **Migration 7 → 8** gives every audio and video object an empty list, at any depth, on slides, layers, masters and layouts. Legacy fixture `v7-two-slides`, plus a unit test of the migration on a synthetic v7 project that has audio and video in it.
- **Editing** goes through the existing `object.update` command (the whole list at once), so undo and redo work as for any property.

## Captions text (`packages/model/src/captions.ts`, pure)

- **`parseCaptions(text)`** reads WebVTT and SubRip and returns `{ cues, problems }`.
  - It accepts a BOM, CRLF line endings, cue identifiers, `HH:MM:SS.mmm` and `MM:SS.mmm`, and SRT's comma.
  - It ignores cue settings and NOTE/STYLE/REGION blocks.
  - Markup is kept as written text: VTT `<i>` and the like are removed, and entities are decoded.
  - A cue it cannot read is skipped and reported in `problems` with its line number. A file with no cues at all is an error.
- **`formatVtt(cues)`** and **`formatSrt(cues)`** write them back. A round trip gives the same cues.

## Runtime

- **What is showing:** `MediaView.caption` is the text of the cues active at the media's file time, joined with line breaks when they overlap. It is null when none is active, or when the media is stopped or has completed.
  - It does show while the media is paused part way, since the learner may be reading.
  - It does not show for audio still waiting for its place on the timeline.
- **The player:**
  - Shows a **CC** button in its footer when the course has any captions. It is off by default, and pressing it toggles captions for the rest of the visit (`aria-pressed`).
  - While on, the captions of the playing media show in a band at the bottom of the stage: white text on a dark translucent box, scaled with the stage.
  - The band is decoration for the eye: it is hidden from screen readers, which get the media itself.

## Editor: the Captions tab

A new bottom-panel tab, "Captions". For the selected audio or video object it shows:

- **Its waveform,** drawn across the whole file, with each cue as a block over it and a cursor.
  - **Play** and **Pause** play the media from the cursor in the editor, and the cursor follows.
  - Clicking the waveform moves the cursor.
  - Clicking a cue's block selects that cue.
  - **Dragging** (added after the first merge, at Luke's request):
    - a cue's edge (within 6 px) moves that edge;
    - a cue's middle moves the whole cue, keeping its length;
    - anywhere else, the cursor moves.
    - Each drag is one undo step, clamped like the time fields (`caption-drag.ts`).
    - Where one cue ends as the next starts, the press goes to the cue on the side it landed on. For overlapping cues, the chosen cue wins.
    - The pointer shows a resize or grab cursor over edges and middles.
  - **Keys:** the waveform is a focusable slider. Left and Right move the cursor 0.1 s (1 s with Shift); Home and End go to the start and end.
- **A video** shows its picture beside the waveform, and that is what Play plays. A video with no sound track has nothing to draw: the waveform shows a dashed line and a note saying so, and playing the picture still moves the cursor.
- **The cues as rows:** start, end (seconds) and text, editable, with Delete. The row of the cue under the cursor is marked.
  - Time fields apply as they are typed (`NumericInput`). Each moves only its own end, kept inside the file and at least 0.05 s from the other end. Typing "2.5" one digit at a time never pushes the other end out.
  - The saved list is always in time order, but the rows hold their places while a time field has focus, and re-sort when it is left. Otherwise the row being typed in would move away from the typing and lose focus.
- **Add caption:** a new cue from the cursor for 2 s (clipped to the file's end), with its text box focused.
- **Import captions…:** VTT or SRT, through the main process, up to 2 MB. It replaces the object's cues as one undoable step, without asking; the notice says how many were replaced and that undo brings them back. Problems are shown, cues starting past the file's end are dropped and reported, and cues running past it are cut at the end.
- **Export captions…:** a save dialog, SRT for a `.srt` name and otherwise VTT (adding `.vtt`). Cues with no words yet are left out.

With nothing, or something other than audio or video, selected, the tab says what to select.

## Done when

- **Model:** parsing VTT and SRT (the cases above, and bad input), formatting, round trips; cue validation; migration 7 → 8; a property-test step that sets captions.
- **Engine:** the caption at a time, overlapping cues, a trimmed video's offset, nothing when stopped or completed, shown while paused.
- **Player browser tests:**
  - no CC button without captions;
  - CC shows the right text as the media plays, and hides it when switched off;
  - the setting lasts across slides.
- **e2e:**
  - insert a sound, import a VTT, edit a cue, add one at the cursor (moved by clicking the waveform), delete one;
  - export SRT and check the file;
  - save and reopen;
  - publish, then turn on CC and see the caption.

## Not in this package

Caption styling options, more than one language per media, captions for slides without media (author notes as captions), automatic transcription, and sending VTT tracks to the browser's own captions.
